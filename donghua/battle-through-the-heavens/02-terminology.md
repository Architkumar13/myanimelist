# 02 · Terminology

[← Back to index](README.md)

The official English subtitles keep the pinyin for realms (Dou Zhe, Dou Huang, Dou Sheng) and translate most other names. The fan wiki and the novel's English translation often use different words. This table lists the subtitle form first, then the others. "First used" is the first episode whose subtitles use the term, from the episode reviews (EP 1–281, continuous numbering). Where a term appears only in a recap or a name, that is said.

A warning about tracks: EP 248–255 and EP 257–273 come from rough machine-like English (some burned in and read by OCR), and EP 274–275 from the Indonesian fan track. Those episodes use other words for the same things ("Martial Saint", "Soul Temple", "Tianfu Alliance"). The rough-track forms are listed under "other translations".

For ranks, tiers, the beast scale, the soul realms and the full flame ranking, see [01-power-system.md](01-power-system.md).

## Notes and doubts

- ⚠ **"Emperor Flame" in EP 185** is a person: "Emperor Flame was the last Dou Di", and the "jade of Emperor Flame" is the key to his mansion. That matches Tuo She Gu Di and his jade, not the first-ranked flame (帝炎) of the wiki. The site treats EP 185's phrase as another name for the jade of Tuo She Gu Di.
- **Rock Gate and Pan's Gate** are one faction. 磐 (pán) means a great rock, which is why the track uses both.
- **Hall of Souls and Soul Shrine** are one faction. EP 32's "Soul Shrine" item is probably the same name.
- **Yan Xiao and Yao Yan.** Xiao Yan's alias in the capital is "Yan Xiao" (EP 37–58, his name flipped). With Yun Yun, and in the Black-Corner Region, he is "Yao Yan" (EP 26, EP 70).
- **Three Thousand Lightning Movement:** first named in EP 71 (the auction), first trained in EP 89–90. Where Xiao Yan got the scroll is never shown.
- 汉字 are left blank where neither the wiki nor a clear novel equivalent gives them (Acceptance Soul, Thawing Spirit Pill, Mighty Huang Pill and a few others).
