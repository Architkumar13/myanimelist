# 04 · Xiao Yan

[← Back to index](README.md)

萧炎. Youngest son of Xiao Zhan, patriarch of the Xiao Clan of Wu Tan City. He became a Dou Zhe at 11, then lost his Dou Qi for three years, and the clan called him trash (EP 1). By EP 281 he is a Five-Star Dou Sheng, the continent's number one alchemist (EP 276) and Young Alliance Master of the Sky Mansion Alliance. Aliases: Yao Yan (to Yun Yun, EP 6), Yan Xiao (in the capital and later, EP 37 on). The realm-by-realm table is in [01-power-system.md](01-power-system.md); every date here comes from the same episode reviews. The places and factions are in [03-world-and-powers.md](03-world-and-powers.md).

His realm label rarely tells you how hard he hits. The gap is borrowed power (Yao Lao, then Tian Huo Zun Zhe), the Sky Fire Three Mysterious Changes, fused Heavenly Flames, puppets and pills.

## Doubts
