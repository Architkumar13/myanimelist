# 01 · Power System

[← Back to index](README.md)

Names first are the ones the official English subtitles use (the Yuewen Animation channel). Other translations follow as "also called". Episode numbers are the site's continuous numbering (EP 1–281; see `data/episodes/REVIEW-NOTES.md`). Things the donghua has not reached are marked **novel only**. Where the fan wiki (which follows the novel) disagrees with what the episodes show, the episodes win and the line is marked ⚠.

Everything rests on **Dou Qi** (斗气), the fighting energy of the Dou Qi Continent (斗气大陆). You raise it with a **technique** (功法, the "Qi Method"; the Flame Mantra is one) and you fight with **Dou Skills** (斗技). Alchemy is a second career that runs beside fighting, and it is where most of the money and favours in the story come from. The soul has its own ladder, and from Season 5 on it matters as much as Dou Qi.

## 1. The Dou Qi ladder

Every rank from Dou Zhe up has **stars**, 1 to 9: "a Three-Star Da Dou Shi". At nine stars you can try for the next rank. The gap between stars, and between ranks, grows as you climb. The subtitles also use **peak** (top of a star or rank), **Half-Step** (半步, at the door of the next rank: "Half-Step Dou Ling" EP 79, "Half-Step Dou Zong" EP 114), and from Dou Sheng on **early / mid / late stage** inside a star (EP 243–249).

Dou Zhi Qi has **stages** instead of stars, first to ninth.

## 3. Dou Skills and techniques: Tian, Di, Xuan, Huang

Four classes, highest first: **Tian** (天, Heaven), **Di** (地, Earth), **Xuan** (玄, Black or Mysterious), **Huang** (黄, Yellow). The subtitles explain them as "Heaven, Earth, Black, Yellow" in EP 5. The order comes from the first line of the *Thousand Character Classic*, 天地玄黄 (wiki). Each class has **low, medium and high rank** (低级、中级、高级; the subtitles also say "advanced" for high). The same scale grades **techniques** (Qi Methods), which raise Dou Qi, and **Dou Skills**, which spend it.

The wiki puts the real wall between Xuan and Di: a Xuan skill runs on the user's strength, a Di skill draws on the world's energy. The episodes back this up. Onlookers rate Flame Splitting Tsunami "at least the Di Class" in EP 59 as if that alone were frightening.

|---|---|

**The Flame Mantra** (焚诀, *Fen Jue*) is Xiao Yan's technique and the reason the story chases flames. It starts at Huang low and evolves each time it swallows a Heavenly Flame: up one level with the Purple Flame (EP 11), to Xuan medium with the Green Lotus Core Flame (EP 28), Di Class by EP 215's recap. It must reach Tian Class before Yao Lao can be given a new body by it (EP 28). It does not reach Tian in EP 214. Han Feng carries an incomplete copy (EP 105).

Other skill words: **Flying Dou Skill** (wings made by a skill, slower than true Dou Qi Wings; Purple Cloud Wings EP 24, named again EP 66; the Gold Geese Sect's method EP 138), **Sonic Dou Skill** (EP 73, Lion Tiger Gold Shattering Roar EP 78), **Fusion Dou Skill** (several people at once, EP 27), **secret technique** (a short boost at a cost, EP 67).

## 4. Alchemists and pills

### Alchemist tiers

Alchemists (炼药师) are graded **tier 1 to 9** by the highest-tier pill they can refine. The subtitles say "tier-6 alchemist", "sixth-tier", "rank 6", "6th grade" and "Sixth Ranked" for the same thing. Some tiers are split into **low, middle and high rank**: "seven-tier middle rank" and "six-tier high rank" (EP 186), "eight-tier middle-rank" and "eight-tier high-rank" (EP 242). Ninth tier is split three ways by the kind of ninth-tier pill you can make (EP 276): **Treasure Pill** (宝丹), **Xuan Dan** (玄丹, also "Mystic" or "Profound" Pill) and **Golden Pill** (金丹). No Golden Pill master has appeared in a thousand years (EP 276). The wiki adds a legendary Di grade above Golden Pill: **novel only**.

An alchemist needs a fire attribute with a trace of wood, and above all a strong soul (wiki). An ordinary alchemist's flame is Dou Qi turned to fire: pale yellow at the start, darker as the tier rises (EP 2).

## 6. The soul

The soul is **Soul Power** or **Spiritual Strength** (灵魂力量) in the subtitles; scanning with it is **Soul Perception** (灵魂感知; EP 1). It decides how good an alchemist you can be. The realms are named in EP 186:

| Soul realm | 汉字 | Wiki's note | In the donghua |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mortal Realm | 凡境 | alchemists of tiers 1–7 | the starting point |
| Spiritual Realm | 灵境 | eighth-tier alchemists; the wiki also calls it the Soul State | Xiao Yan may have reached it, EP 186. Dan Chen "once reached the Soul State", EP 193. |
| Heavenly Realm | 天境 | ninth-tier alchemists; early, late and full stages | only a Heavenly-Realm soul can enter Huang Quan's tablet (EP 247) |

Soul strength can also be measured as a **Soul Value** level on a soul testing stone: the old record in the Pill Region was 9th; Xiao Yan shows "more or less 14th level" (EP 188).

Xiao Yan's soul: a breakthrough under pressure during the Alchemist Conference finals (EP 51–52); growth from the Fallen Heart Flame eating a corpse's spirit (EP 150); the Earth Core Soul Essence (EP 192); "Late-stage Heavenly Realm" (EP 247); the peak of the Heavenly Realm after refining the Soul Origin (EP 256); "Late Heaven Realm Grand Perfection" (EP 257); peak Heavenly Realm again in EP 275. Soul techniques: the **Soul Palms** (EP 188), the **Taiyi Soul Technique** (EP 194), a soul avatar (EP 257).

A soul can outlive its body. That is why the Hall of Souls hunts strong souls without bodies (EP 70, EP 129), and why a Dou Zun "has a chance of resurrection as long as the soul remains" (wiki).

## 7. Bloodlines and clan marks

Introduced at the Gu Clan's coming-of-age ceremony (EP 219):

- **Bloodline ranks** 1–10. Fourth rank or higher to take part. Ninth rank gives a chance at Dou Sheng. Tenth is a **divine-grade bloodline**; Xun Er tests at tenth (EP 220).
- **Clan mark** (族纹) tiers: Silver, Gold, Purple-Gold, Rainbow-Gold (EP 219). The Xiao Clan's mark has nine strokes; Xiao Yan completes the ninth in EP 225.
- Xiao Yan ends up with three bloodlines fused: Xiao (from Xiao Xuan), part of Xun Er's Gu bloodline, and the Dragon-Phoenix bloodline (EP 225–226).

## 8. The Heavenly Flame Ranking

**Heavenly Flames** (异火, literally "strange flames") are flames born from the world itself: in a lotus deep in the earth, in an abyss, in empty space. "Heavenly Flames reign above all flames" (EP 50). A body normally holds only one; the Flame Mantra lets Xiao Yan hold and fuse several (EP 108, EP 110). The ranking is the **Heavenly Flame Ranking** (异火榜; named EP 2). The rough tracks also call it the "List of Strange Fires", "Demon Flame List", "Divine Flame List" and "Strange Flame List". The novel ranks 23 positions; the subtitles never give the total.

To swallow a low flame (rank 16 or lower, wiki) Yao Lao's method needs three things: the **Blood Lotus Essence Pill** for the outside of the body, the **Ice Spirit Cold Fountain** for the inside, and the **Acceptance Soul** to hold the flame until it is refined (EP 11). Higher flames need more: the Sixth-Tier **Ground Spirit Pill** for the Fallen Heart Flame (EP 71, EP 95).
