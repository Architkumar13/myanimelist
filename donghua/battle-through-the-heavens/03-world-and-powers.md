# 03 · World and Powers

[← Back to index](README.md)

Everything here is the **Dou Qi Continent** (斗气大陆). There is no upper world in this story: the continent is the whole board, and the top of it is the Central Plains. Episode numbers are the site's (EP 1–281, numbered straight through; see `data/episodes/REVIEW-NOTES.md`). Names are the official English subtitles' first, other translations as "also called". Realms are in [01-power-system.md](01-power-system.md). Xiao Yan's own gains are in [04-xiao-yan.md](04-xiao-yan.md).

⚠ marks a place where the fan wiki (which follows the novel) and the episodes disagree. The episodes win.

## Factions by tier

Each entry: who leads, the strongest people with the realm the episodes state, what it is known for, and its history with Xiao Yan through EP 281.

### Small clans and mercenary companies

**Xiao Clan** (萧族), Wu Tan City. Patriarch Xiao Zhan, 6-star Da Dou Shi (EP 3). Xiao Yan's family. The elders scorn him as trash (EP 1). Attacked by masked men, Xiao Zhan taken (EP 62). Moved to Rock Desert City (EP 63), ambushed again there by the Misty Cloud Sect and the Hall of Souls (EP 96), sheltered by the Mite'er family in the capital (EP 109, EP 122). Its bigger history: once a top Central Plains power under Xiao Xuan, with a Dou Di bloodline that dried up (EP 185, EP 211, EP 217); one of the Eight Ancient Clans (EP 209, EP 217).

**Jia Lie Clan**, Wu Tan City. Jia Lie Bi, peak Da Dou Shi. Rival family with the alchemist Liu Xi. Attacks the Xiao Residence and loses its leader to Xiao Yan's Octane Blast (EP 3). **Ao Ba Clan** (Ao Ba Pa): its ally; tries to take the Xiao markets in EP 62 and loses them in EP 63.

**Wolf Head Mercenary Company** (狼头佣兵团), Magical Beast Mountain Range. Mu She, 2-star Dou Shi; Gan Mu, 9-star Dou Zhe; He Meng, 8-star Dou Zhe (EP 4). Hunt Xiao Yan for a treasure-chest key. He kills He Meng (EP 5), Gan Mu (EP 8) and Mu She (EP 9).

**Bloody Battle Mercenary Company**, Qingshan Town. Ka Gang, Ling Er, Qing Ye. Friends from EP 8. Saved again from the Snake Nest Mercenary Company and the He Clan (EP 130–132); the He Clan (He Qian) bows and joins the Yan Alliance (EP 132).

**Thousand Medicine House**, Qingshan Town. Mr. Yao, who wants Little Fairy Doctor's Rainbow Poison Book and loses to her poison (EP 9).

**Desert Metal Mercenary Company**, Rock Desert City. Founded by Xiao Ding and Xiao Li as a safe place for their brother (EP 15). Ally throughout. Its rivals: the **Sand Mercenary Company** under Luo Bu, a Da Dou Shi (EP 16), who kidnaps Qing Lin for the Mo Clan (EP 28–30) and mutinies (EP 34).

**Mo Clan of Yan City**. Mo Cheng, "Executioner Mo", External Deacon of the Misty Cloud Sect; his man Mo Ran, 1-star Da Dou Shi (EP 29). Human-and-beast experiments in an underground lab. Xiao Yan and Hai Bodong expose him at his birthday banquet (EP 31) and Xiao Yan kills him with the first Angry Buddha Lotus Flame (EP 32). This killing is the charge the Misty Cloud Sect later lays on him (EP 58).

### The Jia Ma Empire's powers

The empire's ranking of its **Top Ten** strongest is a list of Dou Huang and near-Dou Huang (EP 14, EP 22, EP 42). The wiki lists Yun Yun, Hai Bodong, Jia Xing Tian, Fa Ma, Gu He, Nalan Jie, Mu Chen, Mite'er Tengshan, Yan Shi and Feng Li.

**Misty Cloud Sect** (云岚宗; fan name Yun Lan Sect). Founder Yun Po Tian (EP 60). Old leader **Yun Shan**, who breaks through to Dou Zong in seclusion (EP 60) with the Hall of Souls' help (EP 125). Leader **Yun Yun**, Dou Huang (EP 6, EP 34). First Elder Yun Leng and elders Yun Lei and Yun Sheng, Dou Wang (EP 63, EP 69). Yun Du and Yun Cha are lifted to Dou Huang by Hall of Souls techniques (EP 122). Known for wind techniques and the Covering Sun Cloud Formation, and for the Gate of Life and Death. Ling Ying calls it "a second-rate faction in the Dou Qi Continent" (EP 60), Yao Lao "the weakest among the first-class factions" (EP 67), but it is the strongest faction in the empire (EP 120).
- EP 1: Nalan Yanran, Yun Yun's disciple, breaks her engagement; the Three-Year Agreement.
- EP 31: drops its support for Mo Cheng.
- EP 57–58: Xiao Yan beats Nalan Yanran at the sect. Yun Leng accuses him of Mo Cheng's murder; three Dou Wang elders attack.
- EP 59–66: Hai Bodong, Ling Ying and the python fight for him; Yun Shan comes out; Xiao Yan kills Yun Leng (EP 64); Yao Lao wakes and fights Yun Shan in his body (EP 64–65). Xiao Yan escapes with a tracking mark and goes on the sect's wanted list (EP 66).
- EP 68–69: Yun Lei boasts of breaking Xiao Zhan's limbs. Yun Yun lets Xiao Yan go.
- EP 86–87: secret ties to the Hall of Souls, unknown even to Yun Yun.
- EP 96: attacks the Xiao Clan at the Desert Metal Mercenary Company. EP 110: Yun Shan removes Yun Yun as leader.
- EP 120–127: Xiao Yan comes home with Black-Corner allies, breaks the siege of the Mite'er family (EP 122–123), stops Yun Yun's forced wedding to Gu He (EP 125) and kills Yun Shan (EP 127).
- EP 128–129: Yun Yun, now leader, faces him; he orders the sect disbanded within a month, and it is. Yun Yun leaves the empire (EP 129) and later leads the Flower Sect (EP 213).

**Mite'er family** (米特尔家族; the wiki also writes Primer). Runs the Mite'er Auction House (headquarters in the capital), one of the empire's three big families. Mite'er Tengshan, Dou Wang (EP 38); Hai Bodong is one of them (EP 37). Ya Fei rises from Acting Supervising Elder (EP 36) to head of the family (EP 121). Ally from EP 35: runs the Xiao Clan's stores (EP 63), becomes the Misty Cloud Sect's enemy for helping him (EP 69), shelters the Xiao Clan (EP 109), besieged (EP 122), first seat of the Yan Alliance (EP 130).

**Hai Bodong**, the Ice Emperor. Once one of the Top Ten; a Dou Huang sealed at Dou Ling by Medusa (EP 14). Seal broken by Yao Lao's Breaking Adversity Pill (EP 29); 2-star Dou Huang, down from 5-star (EP 30). Xiao Yan's bodyguard for a year (EP 30–61). Gets his Spiritual Recovery Purple Pill in EP 123. Dou Zong by EP 198, peak Dou Zong by EP 226. The side story *Song of Desert* (EP 18–20) is his past with Medusa's twin sister.

**Nalan Clan** (纳兰家族), capital. Nalan Jie, the Lionheart Commander; his Searing Poison holds "pure energy built up by a strong Dou Wang" (EP 39). Nalan Su, Nalan Yanran's father. Xiao Lin (Xiao Yan's grandfather) saved Nalan Jie, which made the betrothal (EP 54). Xiao Yan, masked as Yan Xiao, cures Nalan Jie for the Seven Magical Green Spirit Saliva (EP 37–44). Joins Xiao Yan's side in EP 124, the Yan Alliance in EP 130.

**Mu Clan**, capital. Head Mu Chen; Mu Zhan, Da Dou Shi (EP 43). Serves the imperial family (EP 68). Rival over Ya Fei (EP 43–46); ally from EP 124.

**Jia Ma Imperial Family**. Guardian Jia Xing Tian, 8- or 9-star Dou Huang (EP 45), one of the Top Ten. Princess Yao Ye (general) and Princess Yue Er (third-tier alchemist). Guardian beast the Shock-Wave Dragon; treasure the Cyan Cauldron (EP 50). Watches and tries to recruit Xiao Yan (EP 42–45); stays neutral against the Misty Cloud Sect (EP 68, EP 122); sends Yao Ye's troops in EP 123; joins in EP 124 and backs him in EP 129–130.

**Alchemist Association** (炼药师公会). Headquarters in the capital, chairman Fa Ma; Black Rock City branch under Fu Lanke and Ao Tuo (EP 11). Runs the Alchemist Conference, which Xiao Yan wins as Yan Xiao (EP 46–52), making him an honorary elder (EP 53). Elder Fu Yan, Dou Huang and fifth-tier alchemist (EP 124). Joins in EP 124 for ten fifth-tier alchemists in a decade (EP 130). **Gu He**, the Pill King, sixth-tier, works with the Misty Cloud Sect (EP 21–26, EP 125) until Xiao Yan beats him and he joins the Yan Alliance's Pill Hall (EP 138). Xiao Yan purges the Pill Hall's corrupt alchemists in EP 229.

**Snakemen** (蛇人族; also Snakemen Clan). Ruled by Queen **Medusa**, Dou Huang (EP 20), Dou Zong after the python merge (EP 61). Eight Large Tribes, their leaders Dou Wang (EP 26–27); Hua She Er reaches peak Dou Wang with a technique (EP 23). Exiled to the desert and wish to return to their homeland (EP 24). One queen per generation, from an egg (EP 20). Enemy in EP 21–27, when Xiao Yan takes the Green Lotus Core Flame and the newborn python. Joins the Yan Alliance (EP 136); Medusa runs the Yan Alliance while he is away (EP 177, EP 198, EP 227).

**Yan Alliance** (炎盟). Founded by Xiao Yan in EP 130: Mite'er family, Mu Clan, Nalan Clan, imperial family and Alchemist Association, with an Elder's Council (Hai Bodong first seat). Joined by the He Clan (EP 132) and the Snakemen (EP 136). Survives the three-empire war (EP 133–138) and the Profound Lion Sect's siege (EP 226–228).

### The neighbours

**Poison Sect**, Chu Yun Empire. Leader: Little Fairy Doctor as the Sky Poison Woman, a Dou Zong (EP 146). Leads the three-sect invasion under Hall of Souls pressure (EP 135), then turns (EP 136). Its traitors serve the **Ten Thousand Scorpion Gate** (万蝎门): chief Xie Shan, old master Xie Biyan, peak 4-star Dou Zong (EP 139), beast the Four-Wing Scorpion (Rank Six). Destroyed EP 141–142; Xie Biyan's dying act is the Demon Poison Spot.

**Gold Geese Sect** (Yan Luo Tian, 2-star Dou Zong, EP 134) and **Mulan Valley** (the Three Elders of Mulan, peak Dou Huang, EP 133). Invaders. Beaten EP 134–138.

**Profound Lion Sect**, one of the top ten factions of the North-Western Region. Shi Tian, 6-star Dou Zun (EP 228). Seizes half the region with Hall of Souls backing and besieges the Yan Alliance (EP 226–228). Survivors surrender.

**Sky Serpent Mansion**. Takes Qing Lin, the "Reincarnated Holy Lady", to mend her soul (EP 32) and raises her as its next leader; she flees a forced marriage (EP 206).

### The Black-Corner Region

Ruled by whoever is strongest. Its **Black Ranking** lists the top fighters: third is Han Feng (EP 70), fifth is Fan Ling's father (EP 71).

**Eight Gates** (Yuan Yi, top ten on the Black Ranking), rulers of Black Mark City. **Blood Sect** (Fan Lao, Dou Huang; son Fan Ling, Dou Ling; cold techniques that Heavenly Flames counter). **Black Skeleton's Tomb** (Mo Er Han). **Ground Flame Sect**. Xiao Yan kills Fan Ling (EP 73). The Eight Gates, Blood Sect and Ground Flame Sect follow Han Feng into Jia Nan Academy for the Fallen Heart Flame (EP 103–108). Xiao Yan beats Fan Lao (EP 106) and kills him (EP 112).

**Han Feng**, the Medicine Emperor. Yao Lao's betraying disciple, sixth-tier alchemist, strongest Dou Huang (EP 95), holder of the Sea Heart Flame. Founds the **Black Alliance**, based in Feng City, during the two years Xiao Yan is underground (EP 110–111). Half-Step Dou Zong when Medusa kicks him to death (EP 114); his soul goes to the Hall of Souls. Back as a Dou Zong in Hall of Souls service (EP 148), about 5-star after eating Protector Xuan's power (EP 156). Xiao Yan takes what is left of his soul (EP 156); Yao Lao destroys it (EP 239).

**Xiao Gate** (萧门). Founded by Xiao Li, who took a Life Devouring Pill to jump from Da Dou Shi to Dou Wang (EP 112). Holds Feng City after the Black Alliance falls (EP 116). Wins over the Crazy Lion Sect, Dark Sky Sect and Luo Sha Gate (EP 116). Fights at Black Emperor City (EP 146–149) and Demon Flame Valley (EP 155).

**Demon Flame Valley** (魔炎谷). Founder the Old Ground Demon Ghost, 7-star Dou Zong (EP 151); First Elder Fang Yan, Half-Step Dou Zong (EP 146). Its Flame Creation Skill makes the man-made Life Transforming Flame. Xiao Yan kills Fang Yan and his brothers (EP 149); Bai Lie kills the Old Ground Demon Ghost (EP 152), whose body becomes Xiao Yan's puppet (EP 157).

**Black Emperor Sect**, Black Emperor City (黑皇城). Mo Tian Xing, about 4-star Dou Zong (EP 146); his son Mo Ya, 6-star Dou Huang (EP 145); chief alchemist Qi Shan. Runs the auction where Xiao Yan wins the Bodhisattva Body Transformation Saliva (EP 146–147). Old Ying Shan, a Dou Zong, steals it and gives it back (EP 147–149).

### Jia Nan Academy

Dean Mang Tian Chi, "the only Dou Zun" in the academy (EP 150), away for years, met as "Mr. Mang" in the Void Lightning Pool (EP 216) and at the Gu Clan (EP 219–220). Retired guardians Qian Mu and Bai Lie, at most 9-star Dou Zong (EP 152). Inner Academy First Elder Su Qian, Dou Zong (EP 103–104). Outer Academy vice dean Hu Qian, Dou Huang (EP 103); the other elders are Dou Wang. Students go from the Outer Academy to the Inner Academy through a top-20 qualifier (EP 70). The Inner Academy runs on **Fire Energy**, its currency, spent in the Blazing Qi Refining Tower, where the sealed Fallen Heart Flame's projections temper students (EP 83–86). The **Strong Ranking** is its ladder: Zi Yan is first, Lin Xiu Ya second, Liu Qing third (EP 94, EP 100–101).

Student factions: **Rock Gate** (磐门; the track also says Pan's Gate), founded by Xiao Yan in EP 84; White Gang (Bai Cheng, Fu Ao), Medicine Gang (Han Xian), Mountain Splitting Gang (Liu Qing), Wolf Fang (Lin Xiu Ya), Moon Spirit (Han Yue).

With Xiao Yan: enrolment first place in EP 3; arrives EP 73; Inner Academy EP 78; takes the Fallen Heart Flame from under the tower (EP 104–110). Su Qian keeps his link to Yao Zun Zhe secret (EP 113), leads the academy against the Black Alliance (EP 113–114) and against Demon Flame Valley (EP 151–156). Xiao Yan returns part of the Fallen Heart Flame to the tower (EP 111, EP 154). ⚠ EP 151 says Su Qian "advanced from peak Dou Huang to Dou Zong", though EP 103–104 already call him a Dou Zong.

### The Central Plains

The big human powers are "one hall, one tower, two sects, three valleys, four pavilions" (EP 158): the Hall of Souls; the Pill Tower; the Profound Sky Sect and the Flower Sect; the Burning Flame Valley, Ice River Valley and Sound Valley; the Wind Lightning, Falling Star, Ten Thousand Sword and Yellow Spring Pavilions. The ancient clans and great beast clans stand outside this list and above most of it.

**Pill Tower** (丹塔), Holy Pill City. The alchemy power; entry needs a seventh-tier alchemist (EP 150). Three heads: Chairman Xuan Kong Zi, Chairwoman Xuan Yi, Chairman Tian Lei Zi (EP 194); no realms stated. First Elder Qiu Ling. Its real core is the **Small Pill Tower**, an Elder's Council under Grand Elder Lin, 2-star Dou Sheng (EP 242). Behind it all, the **Pill Tower Ancestor**, a 6-star Dou Sheng whose true form is a pill (EP 262). Holds the Pill Gathering every 30 years and held the Three Thousand Burning Flame in its Star Region. With Xiao Yan: Xuan Kong Zi, an old friend of Yao Chen, drives Mu Gu off him (EP 189); Xiao Yan wins the Pill Gathering (EP 194) and takes the flame (EP 197); the Pill Tower finds Yao Lao's prison (EP 198–199) but won't fight openly. Xiao Yan wins a Small Pill Tower seat (EP 243) and the vote to join the alliance (EP 244). The Ancestor saves him from Hun Miesheng (EP 256, EP 262) and fights Hun Qianmo at Fallen Mountain (EP 266).

The Pill Region's **five major clans** (Ye, Cao, Dan, Bai, Qiu) hold Elder's Seats. Xiao Yan poses as the Ye Clan's son-in-law (EP 186) and wins its assessment (EP 188); the Ye Clan's Sun Fire Ancient Altar cures Little Fairy Doctor's poison (EP 181–182).

**Wind Lightning Pavilion** (风雷阁), Lightning Mountain. Chief Lei Zun Zhe, Dou Zun (EP 172). Northern Pavilion under Fei Tian, at least 8-star Dou Zong (EP 166); Shen Yun, 4-star Dou Zong (EP 162). Disciple Feng Qing Er, Dou Zong (EP 171). Motto: "The wind and the lightning rule over the Central Plains". Owner of the Three Thousand Lightning Movement, which it says was stolen from it (EP 162). Feud EP 161–174 (Shen Yun dies EP 163, the Northern Pavilion elders are beaten EP 165, Feng Qing Er loses EP 173); truce EP 174 on condition that he never teaches the Movement. Sides with the Heaven Demon Phoenix Tribe and the Hall of Souls (EP 203, EP 239). Xiao Yan destroys the Northern Pavilion and kills Fei Tian in Tian Bei City (EP 241).

**Falling Star Pavilion** (星陨阁). Founded by Yao Lao and Feng Xian (Feng Zun Zhe) (EP 174). Yao Lao is its Chief, Elder Feng his deputy (EP 202), Xiao Yan Young Pavilion Chief (EP 203). Takes Xiao Yan under its protection (EP 172); rescues Yao Lao (EP 199–201); survives Hall of Souls attacks (EP 211–212, EP 239–240). Leads the **Sky Mansion Alliance** (天府联盟) from EP 244: the Falling Star Pavilion, Burning Flame Valley, Flower Sect and Pill Tower, with Yao Lao as chief, later joined by the Void Ancient Dragons (EP 273) and the Medicine Clan's survivors (EP 279). It beats the Hall of Souls at Fallen Mountain and is hailed overlord of the Central Plains (EP 267).

**Burning Flame Valley** (焚炎谷). Chief Tang Zhen; Second Elder Wu Chen, 8-star Dou Zong (EP 176); behind them the Flaming Cloud Ancestor, 1-star Dou Sheng (EP 242). Holds the Nine Dragon Lightning Flame (12th) and kept the Sky Fire Three Mysterious Changes (EP 67, EP 175). Xiao Yan refines the Flame Bodhi Pill for Tang Huo Er and takes Wu Chen's ten blows to inherit the full technique (EP 176–177). Tang Zhen protects him in the ruins (EP 204, EP 208). First to join the alliance (EP 242).

**Ice River Valley** (冰河谷). Master Bing He (Bing Zun Zhe), "as strong as Tang Zhen" (EP 184); Tian Shuang Zi, Dou Zun (EP 182); Tian She, peak Dou Zong (EP 181). Makes bodies; the Ice Zun Force. Hunts Little Fairy Doctor's Woeful Poison Body (EP 177–184) until Xun Er and the Gu Clan warn it off (EP 184). Later in the Profound River Alliance (EP 239).

**Flower Sect** (花宗). One of the top two sects. The late leader Granny Hua; acting leader Hua Jin, 4-star Dou Zun; Grand Elder Qing Xianzi, Ban Sheng (EP 242). Xiao Yan's duel makes Yun Yun its leader (EP 213). Ambushed by the Profound Sky Sect (EP 232) and the Hall of Souls (EP 242). Joins the alliance (EP 244).

**Profound Sky Sect** (天冥宗). Old Demon Tian Ming, Ban Sheng (EP 239); Deputy Sect Leader Liu Cang, peak 9-star Dou Zun (EP 232); Yaohua Xiejun, 6-star Dou Zun (EP 212–213). Allied with the Hall of Souls (EP 198). Its Yi Chen loses a duel to Xiao Yan (EP 198); Xiao Yan kills Liu Cang with one punch (EP 232). Tian Ming's **Profound River Alliance** (with the Ice River Valley and Wind Lightning Pavilion) attacks the Xing Realm (EP 239–240) and dies when Xiao Yan comes out a Dou Sheng (EP 241).

**Yellow Spring Pavilion** (黄泉阁; Huang Quan Zun Zhe, Dou Zun; Wang Chen) and **Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion** (万剑阁; Jian Zun Zhe; Tang Ying) appear at the Four Pavilion Grand Meeting (EP 171–172). **Sound Valley** is named only.

Other Central Plains powers met:
- **Han family** of Tian Bei City (Han Tian, 8-star Dou Huang; grandfather Han Fei; Han Yue and Han Xue). Xiao Yan saves them from the **Hong Clan** (Hong Tian Xiao, said 5-star Dou Zong; killed EP 165) and gives them the Northern Region (EP 241).

- **Gold Swallowing Mouse Tribe** of the Heaven Eye Mountain Range (Jin Shi, 7-star Dou Zong by EP 169; Jin Gu, Dou Zong). Lifelong friends after EP 170.
- **Profound Xuan Sect** (Chen Tian Nan). Xiao Yan cripples his son Chen Xian (EP 191) and kills him in the ruins (EP 207).
- **Black Fire Sect** (Zhao Hei, 6-star Dou Zong). Driven off the Ye Clan's altar (EP 181).
- **Cloud Rain Sect**, the two Ghost Scorpion Demons (Di Xie Zi, peak Sixth Turn Dou Zun). Killed EP 231.
- The **spatial trade fair**, run by the Ground Line Gate, Mysterious Space Sect and Reflecting Treasure Mountain (Old Bao Shan, peak Ninth Turn Dou Zun) (EP 230).

### The great beast clans

The Beast Region's three great clans are the Void Ancient Dragons, the Heaven Demon Phoenix Tribe and the Nine Serene Deep Ground Pythons (EP 166, EP 203).

**Void Ancient Dragons** (太虚古龙). The royal clan of Magical Beasts, with Spatial Strength. Split into four warring islands since the Dragon Emperor, Zi Yan's father, vanished (EP 214). East Dragon Island: Zhu Li, Ban Sheng (EP 215); Hei Qing, 7- to 8-star Dou Zun by Xiao Yan's guess (EP 209). The Northern, Western and Southern Dragon Kings are 3-star Dou Sheng (EP 252). Zi Yan wakes as Dragon Emperor (EP 216), 4-star Dou Sheng by EP 254. **Heaven Demon Phoenix Tribe** (天妖凰族). **Nine Serene Deep Ground Pythons** (九幽地冥蟒族). Under the Beast Region, guarding the Nine Serene Spring. ### The ancient clans

**The Eight Ancient Clans**: Hun, Gu, Yao (Medicine), Yan, Shi (Stone), Ling (Spirit), Lei (Thunder) and Xiao (EP 217). Each once produced a Dou Di (EP 217) and each held one piece of the key to Tuo She Gu Di's Dou Di ruin (EP 220). Status inside a clan is set by bloodline purity, tested on a star chart from first to tenth rank, with clan marks in silver, gold, purple-gold and rainbow-gold (EP 219). Their rule: nobody above peak 5-star Dou Sheng acts freely in the outside world (EP 257).

**Gu Clan** (古族), the Gu Realm. Clan Leader Gu Yuan, Xun Er's father (EP 220; realm not stated). Elders Tong Xuan, Gu Nanhai, Gu Dao. The **Black Submerged Army**: eight commanders, four marshals, two Black Submerged Kings, all young Dou Zun; a King must be Ban Sheng (EP 217–218). Chief commander Gu Zhen; marshal Gu Yao, 8-star Dou Zun; seventh commander Ling Quan, 1-star Dou Zun (EP 219). Xun Er was sent to the Xiao Clan to fetch its piece of the key (EP 87); a blood oath binds the two clans (EP 87, EP 185). With Xiao Yan: Xun Er through the academy years; Ling Quan takes her home (EP 102); the elders call the Xiao Clan a dried-up bloodline (EP 217); Mang Tian Chi wins back the Xiao Clan's Heavenly Tomb slot (EP 219–220); Gu Yuan asks for the jade and is refused (EP 220). Ally against the Hun Clan; shelters the Medicine Clan's survivors (EP 280). Xun Er tests as a tenth-rank, divine-grade bloodline (EP 220).

**Medicine Clan** (药族; the fan track and EP 279 say Yao Clan). The alchemists' clan, founded by the Medicine Emperor. Chief Yao Dan, 7-star Dou Sheng; Yao Wanhuo and Old Shennong, 6-star Dou Sheng (EP 274–275). Expelled Yao Lao as a weak-blooded waste (EP 274). Xiao Yan wins its Pill Gathering and Yao Lao carves his parents' names on the clan monument (EP 276). Destroyed by the Hun Clan and the Void Devouring Flame (EP 277–279); Yao Tian and Yao Ling survive.

**Yan Clan** (炎族): Huo Xuan, high-tier Ban Sheng (EP 257), holder of the Red Lotus Flame and later the Eight Desolations Annihilation Flame; friendly. **Lei Clan** (雷族): warlike, Thunder Realm; Lei Dong, late 3-star Dou Sheng (EP 281). **Stone Clan** (石族): vanishes with its territory (EP 264). **Ling Clan** (灵族; also Spirit Clan): seals its realm (EP 226) and vanishes (EP 239). The Yan, Medicine and Lei Clans form a Three-Clan Alliance without the Gu and Hun (EP 264). By EP 281 only the Gu, Lei and Yan are left besides the Hun.

### The Hall of Souls and the Hun Clan

**Hall of Souls** (魂殿; the early tracks say Soul Shrine). Hunts and harvests strong souls (EP 70, EP 129). Hidden branches across the continent; it ranks beside the Pill Tower (wiki). Structure as the episodes reveal it:
- Protectors in three grades, Man, Earth and Sky (EP 142). Protector Wu, Earth Protector, 8-star Dou Zong (EP 183); Protector Tie, Man Protector, Dou Zong (EP 140); Protector Xuan.
- Honorable Elders, Dou Zun: Mu Gu, eighth-tier alchemist (EP 189); Qing Hai Zun Zhe, 2-star Dou Zun (EP 183).
- Nine Tianzun, at least 5-star Dou Zun (EP 199): First Tianzun (killed EP 255); Second, Gu You, Ban Sheng (EP 238); Third, Ban Sheng (EP 240); Fourth, the Blood River Elder, peak 9-star Dou Zun (EP 228); Zhai Xing, 5-star Dou Zun (EP 211); Eighth, Qiu Di; Ninth (killed EP 228).
- Heaven, Earth and Human Halls plus 24 Earth Fiend Halls (EP 254). Deputy Hall Master, 3-star Dou Sheng (EP 242, EP 256). Hall Master Hun Miesheng, 5-star Dou Sheng (EP 262).

History with Xiao Yan: helped Han Feng against Yao Lao long ago (EP 126); behind the Misty Cloud Sect (EP 86, EP 125); holds Xiao Zhan (EP 125, EP 201, EP 244); takes Han Feng's soul (EP 114) and Yao Lao's (EP 128). Xiao Yan breaks the Death Soul branch and frees Yao Lao (EP 200–201), drives them from the Falling Star Pavilion (EP 211–212), the Yan Alliance (EP 228) and the Pill Gathering (EP 191–196), raids the Human Hall and kills the First Tianzun (EP 255–256), and beats Hun Miesheng at Fallen Mountain (EP 266–267). After that it pulls back all its branch halls (EP 273).

**Hun Clan** (魂族; also Soul Clan). The ancient clan behind the Hall of Souls (EP 209). Chief **Hun Tiandi** (魂天帝; the official track also says Emperor Hun Tian, Soul Emperor). Hun Qianmo, 6-star Dou Sheng, who helped ambush Xiao Xuan (EP 265); Hun Xuzi, 6-star Dou Sheng, its alchemist and spy (EP 275); the Four Demon Saints, about 7-star Dou Sheng (Hun Sha, Hun Yan, Hun Jing, Hun Tu; EP 279); the Soul Demon Elder, 5-star (EP 263); heirs Hun Feng, 2-star Dou Sheng (EP 257), Hun Yu, Hun Ya and Hun Li. Allied with the **Void Devouring Flame**, second on the ranking, which hid the whole clan by swallowing its realm (EP 281) and released the extinct Devouring Spirit Clan (EP 278). With Xiao Yan: its ancestors killed Xiao Xuan (EP 218, EP 224); Hun Ya and Hun Li die in the Heavenly Tomb (EP 225); it holds Xiao Zhan in the Realm of Souls (EP 241); it destroyed the Ling, Stone and Medicine Clans for their bloodlines and jade pieces, and holds four pieces of the jade (EP 281).

## Doubts

- The Black-Corner Region's direction from the Jia Ma Empire (south in EP 69, east in the wiki) and Jia Nan Academy's place (Black Rock City in EP 3's narration only).
- Rock Desert City and "Shi Mo City" are treated as one city; the reviews never say so.
- Tuo She Gu Di: 10,000 years ago in EP 87, one thousand in the wiki.
- Su Qian is a Dou Zong in EP 103–104 and "advances" to Dou Zong in EP 151.
- The Hun Clan and Hall of Souls titles blur: Gu You calls himself the Second Tianzun (EP 238), and Hun Miesheng is "Young Clan Leader" in one episode and Hall Master in others (EP 239–240).
- Hun Miesheng is 5-star Dou Sheng in EP 262, an "obedient Seven-Star" after Hun Tiandi rebuilds him in EP 272; this may be a slip.
