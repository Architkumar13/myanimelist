# 04 · Wang Lin

[← Back to index](README.md)

A carpenter's son from a village in the Country of Zhao, called Tie Zhu at home. Poor spiritual roots, no backing, and
one bead. He is not a genius. He wins by patience, by studying a problem until it breaks, and by killing anyone who
wrongs him twice. From EP39 his realm is his clone's; his real body walks the Ancient God path beside it. The realm
ladder is in [01-power-system.md](01-power-system.md). This page is what he has, in the order the donghua gives it.

## What he has, in order

### EP1–17: Zhao and Huo Fen
- **The Heaven Defying Bead** (EP1). Time inside runs ten times faster, its water heals, and it grows by eating the five
  elements. **Situ Nan**, once the strongest man on the planet, lives in it and teaches him (EP2).
- **Centipede venom** clears Sun Dazhu's poison: level 1 to level 14 Qi Condensation in one go (EP3).
- **Seize Foundation** and the **Underworld Ascension Method** (EP7). The yin method's ice flame stays with him.
- **The Extreme Realm** (EP10), born the night Teng Huayuan kills his parents. Unbeatable at his level, capped at
  Nascent Soul.
- His body is destroyed (EP11). Three hundred years as a Soul Swallower in the foreign battlefield give him **Extreme
  consciousness** (EP13). He comes back in **Ma Liang's body** (EP12).
- **Xu Liguo**, a dead Nascent Soul trained into his devil (EP14). The longest-serving companion he has.

### EP18–38: the Sea of Devils and the Ancient God
- A stolen golden core gives him **False Core** (EP18); **Core Formation** in EP21.
- **Li Muwan**, an alchemist he saves and keeps (EP18–24).
- The **death spell** (EP19), and the **Fighting Evil Sect**, which he leads after killing its master (EP24).
- **Restrictions.** Seven years on the Forbidden Mountain and he breaks them all (EP31–32). Tu Si names him the fourth
  Enlightened One.
- **Tu Si's memory inheritance** and one **body reshaping** (EP34–36). Spirit Snatch, Ancient God restrictions, and
  knowledge he keeps using for the rest of the show.
- The **restriction flag** (EP38). Refining it calls a Heavenly Tribulation he can aim at people.

### EP39–61: Nascent Soul, revenge, mortal life
- A **clone** with the Divine Path Art (EP39), to get past the Extreme Realm's cap. Nascent Soul in EP44.
- The **Mosquito Beast** (EP45).
- **Revenge** on the Teng clan (EP47–52). After it he keeps cultivating for its own sake.
- **Thirty years as a mortal woodcarver** in Nan (EP53–58). His killing energy condenses into pearls; he learns what an
  Intent Realm is.

### EP62–94: Soul Formation
- His own **Life and Death Intent Realm** (EP62). The real body sheds the Extreme Realm and becomes a **Two-Star Ancient
  God**.
- **Thunder Toad** (EP63), **God Slaying War Chariot** (EP67), **Zhou Yi's pagoda and Ascendant Crystal** (EP70),
  **Star Compass** (EP71), **two Rain Celestial Swords** (EP72).
- A Reincarnation Fruit makes his real body a **true Ancient God** (EP80).
- The **Soul Refining arts** and a **Hundred Thousand Soul Flag** (EP87). Dun Tian's gifts and a **three-star**
  Ancient God body (EP88).
- Losing Wan'er again brings Life and Death Intent to **great perfection** (EP92).

### EP95–120: Soul Transformation
- **Soul Transformation** under Dun Tian's guard (EP95). The **Billion Soul Flag** (EP96).
- Hongdie's **Kunji Whip** (EP100). His and Wan'er's **life souls** (EP103).
- Situ Nan's **three killing fingers** (EP103).
- All-Seer's sect: **Nine Revolution Immortal Refining Law** (EP106), **Celestial Slaughter Art** (EP108).
- In the Demon Spirit Land: the **Soul Refining Tribe** (EP111), **Qin Lin's palace and immortal wine** (EP114).

### EP121–151: Ascendant
- **Ascendant by reverse cultivation** (EP121). He refuses to cut his feelings; Bei Lou saves him from Heaven's Wrath.
- The **Yellow Springs** as his Dao (EP122). The **Scattered Devil** sealed in his soul (EP124).
- **Immobilization Technique**, **Celestial Brush**, **Immortal-Binding Net** (EP126). The **Ancient Demon
  inheritance** (EP127).
- A **lightning soul**, an **Immortal Guard**, the **Net-Severing Sword Art** (EP131), **Netherworld Force** (EP132).
- In Allheaven: **Wang Ping** (EP138–142), the **Thunder Origin Art** (EP143). He loses his body, the flag and the
  sword formation to the Moonwatcher (EP144). The **bead is complete** and a **lightning body** is forged (EP145).
- **Karma Whip** (EP147), **Third Eye** (EP148), **Eighteen Plum Restriction** (EP150).

### EP152–160: Corporeal Yang
- **Corporeal Yang** (EP152), the **Wither Dao Pair**, the **Earthfold Step**.
- **Call Wind** and the **Collection Pavilion** (EP154).
- The **Heaven Seal** and a **third War Chariot** (EP160).

## What he does not have yet

- **Illusory Yin** on screen. He goes from Ascendant great perfection (EP147) to Corporeal Yang (EP152) off screen.
- The **Nirvana stages**, and then the **Third Step**. All-Seer has spent thirty thousand years failing at it.
- **Li Muwan back.** He carries her life soul and is heading for the Xiang family of Planet Dong Lin, who are said to
  be able to wake the soul in the bead (EP132, EP152).
- The **Heaven Defying space** opened past its first vision (EP145).
