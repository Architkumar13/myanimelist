# 03 · World and Powers

[← Back to index](README.md)

## The stack

Wang Lin's world grows in rings. A village in one country. A planet of countries ranked by their strongest
cultivator. A star system run by an alliance. A second star system run by clans. Above each star system, the ruins of a
Celestial Realm.

The four Celestial Realms, Wind, Rain, Thunder and Lightning, are what is left of one immortal realm. A vision in EP64
shows a single giant palm smashing it. Each now hangs over a star system: Rain over the Alliance Star System, Thunder
over Allheaven (EP132).

## Planet Suzaku (EP1–103)

The **Alliance Star System** is ruled by the **Cultivation Alliance**. It gives a planet to each country that rises to
level 6, with a Cultivation Planet Crystal that holds a life soul from everyone born there. Planet Suzaku went to
Suzaku Country, which drove out the planet's first people, the **Forsaken Immortal Clan**, and sealed them underground.

- **Country of Zhao** (level 3). Heng Yue Sect, Xuan Dao Sect, the Teng clan and a Corpse Yin Sect branch. Nascent Soul
  is the top. Under it run nine spiritual veins fed with mortals' roots (EP47).
- **Huo Fen** and **Xuan Wu** (level 3). Four great sects, the War Shrine Sect among them. The fire beasts drive them
  out in EP16–17.
- **Sea of Devils.** A cold sea of fog with 998 cities, north of Huo Fen and thousands of times its size. Its Chaotic
  Broken Stars hide the Land of the Ancient God.
- **Country of Chu** (level 3). The Cloud Sky Sect. A vassal of the Giant Demon Clan.
- **Four Faction Alliance** (level 4). Baiyun, Shuimo, Qingmu Cliff, Heihun. The Snow Region takes it in EP60.
- **Snow Region** (level 4, then 5). Ice and snow. Wang Lin breaks it down to level 3 in EP89.
- **Suzaku Plain.** The largest landmass. Suzaku Country, the only level 6 country, holds the middle with three great
  sects (Immortal's Path, Heavenly Jade, Earth Soul). Pilu, with the Soul Refining Sect, is on its edge.

## Beyond Suzaku (EP103–132)

The **Kunxu** star region has the Trading Planet. **Planet Tian Yun**, ten times Suzaku's size, belongs to All-Seer's
Heavenly Fate Sect. Off its East Sea a gate leads into the **Demon Spirit Land**, Qin Lin's old abode, now nine demon
countries built around fragments of Ancient Demons. Nobody above Ascendant may enter.

## Allheaven (EP133–180)

The **Allheaven Star System** has no great sects, only families, ranked by how old they are. The **Thunder Celestial
Temple** is the strongest force and controls the way into the **Thunder Celestial Realm**. Allheaven has four domains.
Wang Lin starts on Planet Ran Yun in the north, heals in the poor Southern Domain, and after the Thunder Celestial Realm
falls he lands in the fog of the west (EP159).

## Who is actually strong

Read from the bottom. Each row's ceiling is what the episodes show.

| Tier | Who | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Ancient Gods, Ancient Demons, the Celestials | Ancient Gods by stars (Tu Si, eight); Celestial Emperors at the Third Step (Bai Fan, early Nirvana Void) |
| 6 | Cultivation Alliance, Thunder Celestial Temple | Envoys at Illusory Yin–Corporeal Yang; masters above |
| 5 | Heavenly Fate Sect, Da Lou Sword Sect, Yao family, Huan Clan, Sky Demon Country, Tang family | Second Step: All-Seer, Ling Tianhou (late Nirvana Cleanser), the Blood Ancestor |
| 4 | Suzaku Country and its great sects, the Forsaken Immortal Clan, Ran Yun's clans | Ascendant: Zhu Quezi at great perfection |
| 3 | Soul Refining Sect, Giant Demon Clan, Corpse Yin Sect, Snow Region | Soul Transformation: Dun Tian at the peak |
| 2 | Four Faction Alliance | Soul Formation |
| 1 | Heng Yue, Xuan Dao, Teng clan, Cloud Sky, Fighting Evil, War Shrine | Nascent Soul |

Two things move a power up a row faster than its numbers suggest. Treasures from the old Celestial Realms: Dun Tian's
Billion Soul Flag lets a Soul Transformation fight like a mid Ascendant (EP94). And whoever stands behind it: the
Suzaku has the Cultivation Alliance, and the demon countries have their Ancient Demons.

## Factions that matter most

- **Teng Clan.** Massacres his family (EP10). He ends it 400 years later (EP47–52).
- **Cloud Sky Sect.** Where Li Muwan waits. He takes it over for her (EP44).
- **Suzaku Country.** The planet's owner. Zhu Quezi wants him as a furnace (EP82). Wang Lin ends up holding the title
  and gives it away (EP103).
- **Soul Refining Sect.** Dun Tian makes him heir and guards his Soul Transformation (EP88–96).
- **Heavenly Fate Sect.** All-Seer takes him as a disciple and puts a mark on him to devour later. He tears it out (EP131).
- **Sky Demon Country.** He climbs its army from Commander to Heavenly Demon Envoy (EP112–124).
- **Thunder Celestial Temple.** He poses as its envoy, then beats its envoys to save the Thunder Realm's survivors (EP158).
