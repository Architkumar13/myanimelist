# 02 · Terminology

[← Back to index](README.md)

The official English subtitles on Tencent's YouTube uploads translate word by word, and they are not consistent. The
same realm can have two names in one episode. This site uses the names fans know from the novel translation and the
fan wiki, and keeps the subtitle name beside it.

Qi Condensation counts levels (level 1 to 15). Every realm after it has early, mid and late stages and a great
perfection (大圆满). False Core (假丹) and False Nascent Soul (假婴) are the edges of the next realm.

Situ Nan lists the first seven in EP2. Situ Nan explains what comes after Ascendant in EP103, and All-Seer gives the
three steps in EP106: the First Step runs from Qi Condensation to Ascendant. The Second Step is Illusory Yin, Corporeal
Yang and the three Nirvanas. The Third Step is the true Dao, and All-Seer has failed to reach it for thirty thousand
years.

Wang Lin's childhood name is Tie Zhu (铁柱; subs "Tiezhu"). Situ Nan calls him Xiao Linzi. He travels as Ma Liang
(EP12–36), Zeng Niu (EP62–103), Qing Mu (EP85–88) and Xu Mu (EP133 on).

## Words worth knowing before they are plot

- **Spiritual root** (灵根). The test he fails in EP1. In Zhao the Teng family strips mortals of theirs to feed its veins.
- **Extreme Realm** (极境; subs "extreme state", once "Ji Realm"). What his grief makes in EP10. Nobody at his level can
  match him, but he can never pass Nascent Soul. So he grows a clone (EP39) and later cuts the Extreme Realm out of his
  real body (EP62). It is a cultivation state, not a place.
- **Restriction** (禁制). A flexible formation that follows its maker's thought. Four grades, Heaven, Earth, Mystic and
  Yellow, and a Void level above them (EP150). Wang Lin's first great skill.
- **Soul Devourer / Soul Swallower.** What he becomes as a bodiless consciousness in the foreign battlefield (EP12).
  Wandering souls obey him in the Land of the Ancient God for the same reason (EP33).
- **Devil** (魔头). A soul he trains into a servant. Xu Liguo is the first (EP14).
- **Intent Realm** (意境; subs "artistic conception", "domain"). The insight needed for Soul Formation. A copied one is
  weak. His own is Life and Death (EP62), later Karma (EP135, EP147).
- **Immortal energy and immortal jade** (仙气, 仙玉). What is left of the shattered Celestial Realms. Soul Transformation
  needs large amounts, and immortal jade can only be taken by force (EP94).
- **Cultivation country.** Countries are ranked by their strongest cultivator: Nascent Soul makes level 3, Soul
  Formation level 4, Soul Transformation level 5. A level 6 country owns its planet and holds its Cultivation Planet
  Crystal (修星之晶), which can kill anyone born there below Ascendant.
- **Furnace** (炉鼎). A cultivator whose insight is taken by another. Zhu Quezi keeps them (EP82).
- **Ancient God** (古神). A race of the Gu clan with planet-sized bodies, counted in stars. Wang Lin's real body grows
  one, two, three stars. Ancient Demons (古妖) and Ancient Devils (古魔) are the other two branches (EP121).
- **Heavenly Dao / Heaven's Law** (天道). Comes down to stop Li Muwan's Nascent Soul (EP76, EP92). At Ascendant he
  refuses to cut his feelings and cultivates against it: reverse cultivation (EP119–121).
- **Origin power** (元力). Second Step power that touches law itself. It beats arts until he has his own (EP142).
- **Leaves.** The Forsaken Immortal Clan ranks its shamans by the leaves in their runes. Seven leaves is about Soul
  Transformation; eleven is peak Ascendant (EP77–93).
- **Armour** (甲). The Demon Spirit Land counts demon power in armour: three is Foundation Establishment, thirty
  thousand is Soul Transformation (EP112).
