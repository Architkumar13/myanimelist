# 05 · Life Forbidden Zones & the Dark Turmoil

[← Back to index](README.md)

> ⚠️ **Spoilers.** The Desolate Ancient Forbidden Land shows up early (📺 EP1–50, where Ye Fan and Pang Bo get de-aged). Everything about who actually lives in the zones is mid-to-late novel material.

## What they are

**Life Forbidden Zones** (生命禁区) (subs: "**Forbidden Land**") are regions where **former Great Emperors, Ancient Emperors and Heavenly Venerables** hide from death.

1. Near the end of their lifespan, an emperor **"cuts themselves with a blade"** (自斩一刀), dropping their own realm on purpose to slow their aging.
2. They seal themselves inside **Divine / Immortal Source** (神源) to wait for the **Road to Immortality** (成仙路) to open.
3. After self-severing they sit at about the **peak of the Quasi-Emperor 9th layer**. They can still **"extreme sublimate"** (极尽升华) back to full Emperor power for a short while, at the cost of their remaining life.

These beings are called **Supremes** (至尊).

## The Dark Turmoil (黑暗动乱)

When their life force runs dry, several Supremes wake together and **massacre whole star fields to drink the life essence of billions**, then go back to sleep. These catastrophes are the Dark Turmoils, and they come back every few eras.

Each era's Great Emperor has to hold them back:

| Emperor | What they did against the zones |
|---|---|
| **Void Great Emperor** | Killed Supremes of Undying Mountain and the Sea of Reincarnation, then dragged several down with him when he died. |
| **Heng Yu** | Killed a Supreme in the Primordial Ancient Mine. |
| **Ruthless Great Emperor** | Walked *into* a zone and killed an unrivalled Supreme at the source. |
| **Wu Shi** | Suppressed all seven zones in his era and pinned the Undying Heavenly Emperor's incarnation on the Sacred Cliff. |
| **Azure Emperor** | Struck the Divine Ruins and killed two true holy spirits. |

> This explains the emperor clans' behaviour in the story. In a Dark Turmoil, the Ji and Jiang families revive their emperor weapons with **their own clan members' blood** as the sacrifice.

## The Seven Forbidden Zones

| # | Zone | Chinese | Master / notable Supremes | Notes |

| 1 | **Desolate Ancient Forbidden Land** (subs: "Ancient Desolate Forbidden Land") | 荒古禁地 | **Ruthless Great Emperor** (狠人大帝) | 📺 Appears early: its **sacred fruits** reverse aging, which is how Ye Fan and Pang Bo end up as kids. ## How it ends (endgame spoiler)

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal</summary>

Ye Fan becomes **Heavenly Emperor**, breaks the "one emperor per era" rule and assembles a **Heavenly Court full of emperor-level experts**. He then leads campaigns into the Primordial Ancient Mine, Immortal Mausoleum, Divine Ruins, Sea of Reincarnation, Shangcang and Undying Mountain, killing or permanently sealing their Supremes and **ending the cycle of Dark Turmoils**.

Only **Ruthless's Desolate Ancient Forbidden Land** remains, because its master never fed on the living.
