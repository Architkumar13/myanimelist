# 01 · Power System (Cultivation Realms)

[← Back to index](README.md)

> **Names in (brackets)** are what the **Shrouding the Heavens Animation Official** YouTube channel uses in its English subtitles, checked against the subtitle tracks of EP01–50. For the full list of name variants, see [02-terminology.md](02-terminology.md).

## The core idea

This is a **body cultivation** system — *"take the body as a seed."* Each major realm awakens a hidden "secret realm" (秘境) inside a different part of the body, which is why breakthroughs are so physical. The five foundational realms map to the body like this:

| Realm | Body region | Sub-stages |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| Wheel Sea (Wheel-and-Sea Realm) | Below the navel, Yin-Yang core (Wheel of Life) | Sea of Bitterness (Bitter Sea) → Fount of Life (Life Spring) → Divine Bridge → Other Shore |
| Dao Palace | The five organs, Five Elements (Five Viscera) | Five palaces, cultivated in any order |
| Four Poles (Four Extremes) | The four limbs | Four stages, cultivated in any order |
| Dragon Transformation | The spine | Nine Transformations (each a lightning tribulation) |
| Immortal Platform | The brain / primordial spirit (divine consciousness) | Six "Heavens" |

After these five come **Quasi-Emperor → Great Emperor → Red Dust Immortal**, and then the Immortal Dao realms, which are mostly out of reach in this world.

## The Realm Ladder (low → high)

### 1. Wheel Sea / Sea of Bitterness (轮海) (Wheel-and-Sea Realm) — "the Seed"
Seated below the navel (the "Wheel of Life"). The **Wheel of Life** (命轮) sits beneath the **Sea of Bitterness**. Every year of life leaves a scar on the Wheel, and when it is covered in scars and shatters, the person dies. Cultivating means driving a channel down through the Sea to the Wheel so that Life Qi can pour out and hold that decay back.

Four sub-stages, in order:
1. Find the **Sea of Bitterness** (Bitter Sea) (苦海): Life Qi rises from it and nourishes the flesh, bones and organs.
2. Cultivate the **Fount of Life** (Spring of Life) (Life Spring) (命泉): Life Qi condenses into a divine spring. This is the cultivator's first real **energy source**, which unlocks mystic arts and flying on "divine rainbows" (which are really light flowing from the Spring). Lifespan reaches about **200 years**.
3. Erect the **Divine Bridge** (神桥): a Divine Meridian is forged across the Sea of Bitterness. Body transforms, spirit sublimates.
4. Arrive at the **Other Shore** (彼岸, *Paramita*): the body is reborn, "shedding its shell nine times like a butterfly leaving a cocoon."

> Ye Fan's Bitter Sea refuses to open at first. That's because of his physique, not a lack of talent; see [06-ye-fan-cheats.md](06-ye-fan-cheats.md).

### 2. Dao Palace (道宫)
Awakens the five internal organs (the "Five Viscera"). Five sub-realms, each called a **Divine Treasury / Divine Depository** (神藏) and each tied to one of the Five Elements. The order differs from cultivator to cultivator:

| Divine Treasury | Chinese |
|---|---|
| Heart | 心之神藏 |
| Lungs | 肺之神藏 |
| Liver | 肝之神藏 |
| Spleen | 脾之神藏 |
| Kidneys | 肾之神藏 |

Opening the first treasury raises lifespan past 200 years. Each palace houses an inner "deity" (the subs mention "deities within the five viscera").

### 3. Four Poles / Four Extremities (四极) (Four Extremes)
Awakens the four limbs, the "four pillars that prop up heaven." Four sub-realms, cultivated in any order by choice. Special physiques (e.g. "Sacred Body") often face tribulations here because heaven resents their potential.

### 4. Dragon Transformation (化龙)
Cultivates the spine, bottom to top, on the way up to the Immortal Platform in the head. **Nine sub-levels called Transformations**. Each one requires surviving a **Heavenly Punishment** (lightning tribulation). A major power spike where the body evolves toward the divine. Lifespan reaches about **1,000 years**. (Among demons, a Dragon Transformation cultivator is called a *Great Demon*, 大妖.)

### 5. Immortal Platform (仙台)
Awakens the head and the primordial spirit (元神) (the subs say "where divine consciousness resides"). This is the most mystical realm, where a cultivator first touches immortal aura. It has **six steps called "Heavens" (层天)**, and each Heaven is as strong as an entire earlier nine-stage realm. By the end of this realm a cultivator must have founded their own Great Dao and cultivation method.

| Heaven | Title | What it means | Lifespan |
|--------|-------|---------------|----------|
| 1st | Entry to Immortal Platform; peak = **Half-Step Great Power** (半步大能) | Supreme elders of holy lands and old clans sit here. Divine sense gets honed like a blade. | ~2,000 yrs |
| 2nd | **Great Power / Almighty** (大能), a.k.a. **Holy Lord** level | Can make short jumps through the void. Holy Lords (圣主) and sect masters of big powers. | ~3,000 yrs |
| 3rd | **King** (王者) / **Dao Severing** (斩道); peak = **Half-Saint** (半圣) | You "sever" part of your past (memories, attachments) to make your Dao clear. Divine sense covers a planet. | ~4,000 yrs |
| 4th | **Saint** (圣人) | Life-level sublimation and entry into the Domain of Laws. Can crush planets and travel between stars for real. A "holy barrier" means non-Saints can't fight Saints, except with forbidden arts or at Half-Saint. | ~6,000 yrs |
| 5th | **Saint King** (圣人王) | Deeper law domains. | ~7,000 yrs |
| 6th | **Great Saint** (大圣) | The limit of Saint-level laws. Each minor step equals a whole Dragon Transformation realm. (Demon equivalent: *Demon God*, 妖神.) | ~8,000 yrs |

*(Fan translations shift these by a step here and there, but this is the standard breakdown.)*

### 6. Quasi-Emperor (准帝)
A "pseudo-7th Heaven." The cultivator re-cultivates and perfects the **Imperial Scripture** they created. It has **nine heavenly layers** (准帝一重天 … 九重天). At each layer you re-run all five earlier realms (Wheel Sea → Dao Palace → Four Poles → Dragon Transformation → Immortal Platform) and survive a Quasi-Emperor Tribulation. Lifespan reaches about **9,000 years**.

- **Alternative Enlightenment** (另类成道): a peak Quasi-Emperor who reaches emperor-like power *without* the Heavenly Heart Seal, which is possible when someone else already holds it in that era. They're stronger than a 9th-layer Quasi-Emperor but weaker than a true Great Emperor.

> The ancient beings sealed inside the **Forbidden Zones** (Forbidden Lands, e.g. the Ancient Desolate Forbidden Land), called **Supremes (至尊)**, sit around this tier. They are ex-emperors who "cut themselves down" to the peak of the 9th Quasi-Emperor layer to live longer, but they can briefly flare back to full Emperor power. See [05-forbidden-zones.md](05-forbidden-zones.md).

### 7. Great Emperor (大帝)
The **apex of mortals, one per era.** You fuse the five secret realms perfectly into one (a "Divine Fetus Dao Fruit"), merge with the mark of Tianxin (**Heavenly Heart Seal**, 天心印记), and become the emperor. Lifespan is roughly **10,000 years** (10,000–20,000 depending on foundation and race). Among non-human races the same rank is called **Ancient Emperor / Ancient Sovereign** (古皇), and in the mythic era, **Heavenly Venerable** (天尊).

> A rare sub-tier, **Heavenly Emperor (天帝)**, is an *invincible* Great Emperor whose strength can overwhelm several ordinary ones, but that's a late-story concept.

### 8. Red Dust Immortal (红尘仙)
The **ceiling of this world.** A Great Emperor must live **nine lives** to break through into a Red Dust Immortal, roughly equivalent to a Quasi-Immortal King. The method was pioneered by Heavenly Emperor Huang (荒天帝) of the earlier novel *Perfect World*.

### 9. True Immortal → Immortal King → Immortal Emperor (仙 / 仙王 / 仙帝)
The **Immortal Domain** itself, reached via the Immortal Road (Road to Immortality). The top of the cosmology, and mostly the stuff of legend until very late in the story. (These belong to the shared universe of Chen Dong's *Perfect World* → *Shrouding the Heavens* → *Sacred Ruins*.)

## Weapon grades (matches the realm ladder)

Weapons are graded by the realm of whoever forged or empowered them:

Wheel-Sea / Dao Palace / Four Poles / Dragon Transformation grade → **Great Power / Holy Lord weapon** → **King weapon** → **Saint weapon** (圣兵) → **Great Saint weapon** → **Quasi-Emperor weapon** (准帝器) → **Imperial / Emperor weapon** (帝兵, "Imperial Weapon" in the subs) → **Immortal weapon** (仙器)

An **Extreme Dao Imperial Weapon** (极道帝兵) is a Great Emperor's personal weapon. Owning one is what separates an "imperial clan" from everyone else. The subs have a line saying a mere ten-thousandth of such a weapon's power can wipe out a force.

## The catch (and the whole theme)

The Shrouding the Heavens world is a **damaged, degraded fragment** of a once-perfect world. Its channel to the Immortal Domain is broken and can't accept normal cultivators, so the road to true immortality is essentially walled off. That "broken road to immortality" is the tragic engine of the entire story. Even Great Emperors, the mightiest mortals who ever live, eventually **wither and die**, because real immortality is out of reach. The desperate ones seal themselves in the Forbidden Zones and wait, and that's where the *Dark Turmoils* come from.
