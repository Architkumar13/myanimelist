# 02 · Terminology — Official Subs vs Fansubs vs Novel

[← Back to index](README.md)

The same Chinese term gets translated several ways. This page lists every variant I've found so you can match what you hear on screen to what the other pages use.

**Columns:**
- **This doc**: the name used across these notes.
- **Official YouTube subs**: what *Shrouding the Heavens Animation Official* uses, taken from the English subtitle tracks of **EP01–50** (the free episodes). "—" means the term doesn't come up in those episodes.
- **Other translations**: fansubs, the English novel translation and the fan wiki.
