## 0. The core "cheat": Ancient Desolate Sacred Body (荒古圣体)

*Other names: Desolate Ancient Holy Body, Holy Physique.*

**Why it's a cheat**
- 📺 **Golden, ocean-sized Bitter Sea**, "hard as meteoric iron" (as the subs put it). Normal cultivators have a pond by comparison.
- 📺 Monstrous physical strength, endurance and recovery. The longer he fights, the stronger he gets.
- 🎬 At Four Poles his **blood turns golden**, which is itself a priceless medicine. His body reaches the **Minor Completion "Indestructible Golden Body."**
- 📖 Every realm can later be **re-cultivated** to go deeper (Sacred Bodies can "specialize" in old realms at Saint and above).
- 📖 Final form: **Great Perfection Sacred Body**, which can fight Great Emperors.

**Why it's also a curse**
- 📺 It needs **absurd amounts of resources**. Everyone thinks it "can't be cultivated in this era," which is why Lingxu never truly accepts him.
- 🎬 From Four Poles on, **heaven itself rejects it**, with extra-brutal tribulations.
- 🎬 **Great Dao Scar** (大道伤痕): after the Four Poles tribulation his life source is being cut away and he has about six months to live. Healed in the Desolate Ancient Forbidden Land (sacred fruits, Qilin herb, Nannan's seven-coloured stone) around **EP120**. While hiding it he gets the nickname "Ye Hei."
- 📖 **"The Holy Body cannot attain the Dao."** No Sacred Body in history became a Great Emperor; the best stalled at Quasi-Emperor peak. Breaking that rule is Ye Fan's life goal (see §6).

## 6. Quasi-Emperor → Great Emperor → Red Dust Immortal (📖)

<details>
<summary>Endgame spoilers: click to reveal</summary>

- **Inheritance of a Great Perfection Sacred Body.** He gets the "original technique" of a previous Sacred Body, the key to pushing the physique past its limit.
- **Quasi-Emperor** (ch.~1639–1739), with the **Qian (前) Secret** (foresight).
- **Great Emperor** (ch.~1790). He isn't accepted by Heaven's Dao; he **overpowers it**, which gives him the title **Heavenly Emperor Ye** (叶天帝). He writes the **Heavenly Emperor Scripture** (天帝经), which has no physique restriction, so anyone can use it.
- **Final weapon:** the **Heavenly Emperor Cauldron** (天帝鼎), made by fusing the Mother Qi Cauldron with a nine-colour immortal cauldron (and the Green Copper cauldron).
- **Nine lives → Red Dust Immortal** (ch.~1820). In his 5th life he turns his body into a **Chaos Body** and writes the **Chaos Scripture**; in his 8th he returns to a mortal body. He eventually collects **all Nine Secrets**.
