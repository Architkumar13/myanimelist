## How strong is a "sect"? The faction tier list

Every faction's standing comes down to **how strong its strongest living person is**, and **whether it owns a Great Emperor's weapon/scripture**.

| Tier | Faction type | Examples | Typical leader | Hidden trump card |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Small sect** (门 / 宗) | Qingxia Sect, Qin Sect | Dao Palace → Dragon Transformation | — |
| 2 | **Paradise / Cave Heaven / Blessed Land** (洞天 / 福地) | Lingxu (Spirit Ruins) and the other Yan Kingdom paradises | Master at **Dao Palace**; elders at Divine Bridge / Other Shore | — |
| 3 | **Great sect** ("near holy land") | Taixuan Sect, Zi Wei Sect, Human Desire Dao | Immortal Platform (probably; the wiki doesn't state it) | Rare inheritances (e.g. one of the Nine Secrets) |
| 4 | **Holy Land** (圣地) (subs: "Sacred Land") | Yaoguang, Daoyi, Tianxuan, Wind Clan, Yin-Yang Sect | **Holy Lord = Great Power, Immortal Platform 2nd Heaven**; supreme elders at IP 1st Heaven | Sealed ancestors at **Saint** or even **Great Saint** level; often a Saint weapon or an emperor weapon |
| 5 | **Ancient war clans & ancient countries** | Wang family, Barbarian clan, Land of Tranquility | **King (IP 3rd Heaven)** peak; protectors up to **Saint King** | Great Saint–grade weapons |
| 6 | **Emperor Clans & Immortal Dynasties** (founded by a Great Emperor) | Ji, Jiang, Yaochi, Great Xia, Jiuli | Immortal Platform elders; living legends at **Saint → Great Saint** | **Extreme Dao Imperial Weapon + Imperial Scripture**. Untouchable. |
| 7 | **Life Forbidden Zones** | 7 zones | **Supremes**: self-severed ex-emperors (≈ Quasi-Emperor 9th layer, able to burst to Emperor) | Can start a Dark Turmoil. See [05-forbidden-zones.md](05-forbidden-zones.md). |

**Rules of thumb**
- In the present "Dharma-ending" age, **Great Saints are the strongest beings openly walking around**. No living Great Emperor exists when the story starts.
- A Holy Lord (IP 2nd Heaven) is roughly the ceiling of what a normal big power can field in public.
- A revived **imperial weapon** outclasses any living Saint, which is why emperor clans get their way.
- Tiers 5 and 6 blur. The wiki calls the Wang family "on par with an Immortal Dynasty."

## The map: Beidou (北斗, the Big Dipper star field)

Ye Fan's new world is a single enormous planet in the **Big Dipper** star field, divided into five regions:

```
                 Northern Plains (北原)
                        │
Western Desert ── Eastern Wasteland (东荒) ── (sea)
   (西漠)               │     ← the story starts here (its south)
                 Central Prefecture / Zhongzhou (中州)
                        │
                 Southern Ridge (南岭)
```
*(Rough layout for orientation only. In the novel the Eastern Wasteland is itself huge and split into Southern, Northern and Central regions.)*

## 1. Eastern Wasteland: Southern Region (📺 EP1–50+)

This is the region where the story opens. The mortal **Yan Kingdom** (燕国, capital **Yandu**) sits here, and the **six Paradises of the Yan Kingdom** sit above it. The 13 surviving classmates are split between them.

| Faction | Type (tier) | Strongest people | Signature | Ye Fan & it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lingxu Cave Heaven / Blessed Land** (灵墟洞天) (wiki: "Spirit Ruins Paradise") | Paradise (T2) | **Master: Dao Palace 1st Treasury.** Supreme elders: Other Shore. Elders (e.g. **Elder Wu**, Pang Bo's master): Divine Bridge. Disciples: Bitter Sea → Life Spring. | Dao Scripture (Wheel-and-Sea) fragment | His first sect. He's never formally accepted because his Sacred Body "can't cultivate." **Elder Han** wants to refine him into medicine and dies trying. Ye Fan leaves. |
| **Violet Sun / Ziyang Paradise** (紫阳洞天) | Paradise (T2) | — | — | Li Xiaoman joins. Ye Fan provokes it while escaping. |
| **Golden Sun Clouds**, **Jade Cauldron** (subs: "Yuding Cave Heaven"), **Hazy Light**, **Dusk Moon** paradises | Paradises (T2) | — | — | Other classmates join these (Liu Yiyi → Jade Cauldron, Zhang Ziling → Golden Sun Clouds, Cade → Dusk Moon). Also in the subs: **Yanxia Cave Heaven**. |
| **Yin-Yang Sect** (阴阳教) | Great sect (its HQ is in Central Prefecture, see §4) | — | — | Kidnaps special-physique children around Yandu, **Nannan** among them. Ye Fan drives it out of the region. |
| **Demon race** (Yan Ruyu's group) | Demon clan | — | The **Azure Emperor's heart** | Princess Yan Ruyu forces Ye Fan to nurture the Demon Emperor's heart with his body (**EP39**). |
| **Ji family** (姬家) | **Emperor Clan (T6)**, rules the Southern Region | Founder: **Void Great Emperor**. Elders at Immortal Platform (e.g. Ji Hong); **Ji Hui** at Four Poles; an unnamed Great Saint protector | **Void Scripture**, **Void Mirror** | Long-running enemy, later in-laws. Ye Fan takes **Ji Ziyue** hostage, then escorts her home. The Ji hunt him for knowing the *Great Void Technique*. |
| **Yaoguang Holy Land** (摇光圣地) (subs: "Yaoguang Sacred Land") | Holy Land (T4+) | Holy Son **Hua Yunfei**, plus the later Holy Son **Yao Guang** (reaches King level); saintess **Yao Xi** | **Dragon-Pattern Black Gold Cauldron**, Holy Light arts | Hunts Ye Fan together with the Ji. Ye Fan hides as a mortal miner in Yaoguang's mines and later robs them. |
| **Taixuan Sect** (太玄门) (subs: "Taixuan Sect") | Great sect, 108 peaks (T3) | Hall master Li Ruoyu of **Humble Peak**; Star Peak is the strongest peak | 108 inheritances, **one of the Nine Secrets** | Ye Fan and Ji Ziyue join to learn the secret. | **Qingxia Sect** | Small sect (T1), Dao Palace level | — | — | ⚠ The wiki says Ye Fan destroys it; another page lists it as an affiliation. |

> **Flame Domain / Fire Region** (火域): a location at the western end, not a sect. Ye Fan tempers himself in its layers of fire and later takes **nine-colour fire** from it to refine his cauldron.

| Faction | Type | Strongest people | Signature | Ye Fan & it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Yin-Yang Sect HQ**: Yin-Yang Divine City | One of the "hundred great sects" (T4) | **Supreme Master Wang Yangzhan: Great Power (IP 2nd Heaven, peak)**; six Half-Step Great Powers as guards | **Yin-Yang Mirror** (Great Saint weapon) | Arch-enemy. Wang Yangzhan's dying **Yin-Yang Curse** is the furnace Ye Fan uses to break into **Immortal Platform (EP156)**. He then half-wrecks the city with dragon-vein formations, and Duan De robs its ancestral tombs. |

## 5. Ziwei Ancient Star (紫微古星): another planet (EP~157–170)

The Nine Dragons' coffin carries Ye Fan across the stars to Ziwei. Here **all emperor weapons vanished with their emperors**, so the balance of power is different.

| Faction | Type | Notable people | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Human Desire Dao** (人欲道) | Daoist sect | **Li Tian**, **Yan Yixi** | Allies who return to Beidou with him (**EP160**). Holds the **Goddess Furnace** (made by Heng Yu). |
| **Golden Crow race** (金乌) | Ancient race | Golden Crow King; 6th Crown Prince **Lu Ya** (Great Power) | Ye Fan kills Lu Ya. |
| **Xuandu Cave** / Palace of Eight Visions | Laozi-lineage temple | Yin Tiande | Rival. |
| **Moon Palace**, **Taiyin Palace**, **Longevity Temple**, **Human King Hall**, **Ancient Sky Wolf** | Holy Lands / temples | Yi Qingwu (Moon Palace) and others | Ziwei's great powers. |
| **Ziwei Celestial Dynasty**, **North Sea** kingdom | Dynasty / kingdom | Princess Yueshi; Black Dragon King | — |

> Ziwei is the home star of the Sun and Moon emperors, which is why Ye Fan gets both **True Scriptures** around this arc.
