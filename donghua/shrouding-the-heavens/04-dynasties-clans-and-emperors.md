## 1. Emperor-legacy clans: the real power holders

In this world, **whoever owns a Great Emperor's weapon (帝兵) sits at the top**. A clan descended from a Great Emperor, holding that emperor's **Extreme Dao Imperial Weapon** and **Imperial Scripture**, is called an **Emperor Clan** (帝族 / 圣地世家). Even a Saint won't pick a fight with one, because a revived imperial weapon can wipe out a region.

| Clan / power | Region | Founding Great Emperor | Imperial weapon | Imperial scripture | Strongest people you'll see |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jiang family** (姜家) (subs: "Ancient Desolate Jiang Family") | Northern Region, Eastern Wasteland | **Heng Yu** (恒宇大帝, the Eternal Universe Emperor) | **Hengyu Furnace** (恒宇炉) | **Hengyu Scripture** (恒宇经): its Four Poles volume is the best for that realm | **Divine King Jiang Taixu** (姜太虚), a Saint King–level legend who has been missing for 4,000 years; also Immortal Platform elders |
| **Ji family** (姬家) (subs: "Ji clan") | Southern Region, Eastern Wasteland | **Void Great Emperor** (虚空大帝) | **Void Mirror** (虚空镜) | **Void Scripture** (虚空经): the "Great Void Technique" lets you teleport | Immortal Platform supreme elders; later Ji Haoyue (divine body) and Ji Ziyue |
| **Yaochi Holy Land** (瑶池) (subs: "Yaochi Sacred Land") | Northern Region, Eastern Wasteland | **Queen Mother of the West** (西皇母) | **Immortal-Tear Green Gold Tower** (仙泪绿金塔 / 西皇塔) | **Xihuang Scripture** (西皇经): the best Dao Palace method | Mostly female disciples; the Holy Maiden and the current "Queen Mother" head |

| **Wushi's legacy** (Purple Mountain) | Eastern Wasteland | **Wu Shi** (无始大帝), the last human emperor of the Desolate Ancient era | **Wushi Bell** (无始钟) | **Wushi Scripture** (无始经) (needs his physique) | The **Black Emperor** (黑皇), Wu Shi's dog, is his real "heir" in the story |
| **Daoyi Holy Land** (道一圣地) | Eastern Wasteland | **Dao Yan** (道衍大帝) | Daoyan Divine Cloth | — | — |

> **Why these clans matter to Ye Fan:** each one owns the best scripture for one realm. Ye Fan ends up stitching together the *strongest method for every realm* from different emperor legacies (Dao Scripture → Xihuang → Hengyu → Taihuang → Sun/Moon). See [06-ye-fan-cheats.md](06-ye-fan-cheats.md).

### The Four Divine Dynasties of Central Prefecture (中州)
Central Prefecture is ruled by **four immortal dynasties that have lasted hundreds of thousands of years**, with **nine ancient countries** (70–80k years old) beneath them. Each dynasty holds an emperor weapon, which puts it on the same level as an Emperor Clan.

| Dynasty | Founder | Imperial weapon | Scripture | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Great Xia Dynasty** (大夏皇朝) | **Sovereign Tai** (太皇), who was ambushed and killed by the Undying Heavenly Emperor while trying to ascend | **Tai Sovereign Sword** (太皇剑), half-destroyed in a Dark Turmoil | **Taihuang Scripture** (太皇经): the best Dragon Transformation method; Ye Fan gets it in Central Prefecture | Led by "Human Emperor" **Xia Yiming** (Immortal Platform). Rival of Gu Hua. |
| **Jiuli Divine Dynasty** (九黎神朝) | **Great Emperor Jiu Li** (九黎大帝) (his corpse later becomes **Chiyou** on Earth) | **Jiuli Map** (九黎图), which "contains three thousand worlds" | — | Nearly kills Pang Bo in the Qinling war. Flips between allied and hostile toward Ye Fan, but generally opposes the ancient races. |
| **Gu Hua Dynasty** (古华) | — | Owns a Saint-grade golden human-shaped armor | — | ⚠ One wiki page says it was destroyed in a Dark Turmoil; another lists it as active. |
| *(4th)* | — | — | — | ⚠ Not clearly named in the sources (possibly the Shenzhou dynasty). |

### Assassin Divine Dynasties (杀手神朝)
Three dynasties that grew out of ancient assassin organizations and once went to war with all the holy lands:

- **Heavenly Court** (天庭): wiped out 100,000+ years ago. Its fleeing assassins died inside **Undying Mountain**, and their Killing-Dao arts were lost with them. Ye Fan later recovers the **Celestial Court Killing Dao Holy Technique**.
- **Human World** (人世间) and **Underworld** (地狱): still active. You hire them through contact villages in the Central Region, and the fee scales with the target's status.

Connections to Ye Fan: at the Broken Heaven Mountains, 4 Underworld Lords and 3 Human World Lords ambush him. He kills them with the Black Emperor's **Wushi Killing Formation**. Later, around his Immortal Platform breakthrough, he **destroys both surviving assassin dynasties**.

### Other dynasties (later arcs)
- **Yuhua (Feathered) Divine Dynasty** (羽化神朝): the "most powerful dynasty" of its day. It sacrificed Ruthless's Sacred-Body brother, and she erased it and killed its emperor. Its ancestral temple in Central Prefecture holds the **star map back to Earth**.
- **Ziwei Celestial Dynasty** and the **Golden Crow Dynasty**: on the Ziwei Ancient Star (another planet).
- **Ye Fan's Heavenly Court** (天庭): Ye Fan rebuilds the "Heavenly Court" name with the survivors of the ancient one, and it becomes his own faction. Members include Pang Bo, Ji Haoyue, Tu Fei, Li Heishui, the Black Emperor and others, with the Old Madman (a Great Saint) as protector. By the end, several of them are Quasi-Emperors.

## 3. The great clans that are *not* emperor clans

| Clan | Region | Strength | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wang family** (王家) | Northern Plains | Supreme King–level elders, e.g. the Dark Demon King | Home of **Wang Teng**, who inherited the **Luan Gu (Ancient Chaos) Emperor's** legacy and is Ye Fan's big early rival. (The movie *Battle Against Wang Teng* covers this.) Ye Fan later destroys the clan. |
| **Barbarian clan** (夷部族) | Southern Mountains | Barbarian King (Supreme King peak), Hei Wu (Saint King); owns a Great Saint's axe | Guarded by Azure Dragon, Black Tortoise and White Tiger beasts. Allies with Ye Fan against the Wang family. |
| **Wind clan** (风族) | Central Region, Eastern Wasteland | Holy Lord–level head | Linked to Fu Xi (Fuxi Dragon Stele, Eight Trigrams). |
| **Golden Crow race** (金乌) | Ziwei Star | Immortal Platform princes | Ye Fan kills the 6th Crown Prince **Lu Ya**. |

**Ancient Races / Taigu Royal Clans** (太古王族): Qilin, Blood Phoenix, Myriad Dragons, Great Roc, Golden, Divine Silkworm, and the **Fighting Saint Ape** (斗战圣皇, the "monkey" line). They ruled in the Supreme Ancient era. Their Ancient Emperors sealed themselves inside the Forbidden Zones, and their heirs come back mid-story, which throws the human world into turmoil.

> **Earth connection:** several of Earth's legends are the bodies of Beidou's emperors. Chiyou is Jiu Li, the Yellow Emperor is the Void Emperor, Laozi is Dao De, and Shakyamuni is Amitabha. That's why Ye Fan keeps finding clues to Beidou on Earth.

## 5. Big picture (endgame spoiler)

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal how it all ends</summary>

- Ye Fan breaks the curse that "the Holy Body cannot attain the Dao" and becomes **Great Emperor Ye → Heavenly Emperor Ye** (叶天帝). His weapon, the Mother Qi Cauldron, becomes the **Heavenly Emperor Cauldron** (天帝鼎), and he writes the **Heavenly Emperor Scripture** (天帝经), which has no physique restriction.
- He **ends the "one emperor per era" rule**, so many emperors can rise at once.
- He leads campaigns into six of the seven Forbidden Zones and ends the Dark Turmoils. Only Ruthless's Desolate Ancient Forbidden Land is left standing.
- He lives nine lives, becomes a **Red Dust Immortal**, and enters the Immortal Domain together with Wu Shi, Ruthless and Duan De.
