# 01 · Power system

Jade Dynasty has no single ladder that every school shares. The donghua only spells out one: Qingyun's
**Taiji Xuanqing Dao** (太极玄清道). Every other school runs its own method beside it, and demons and beasts are
measured in years of training. Episode numbers below are the first time a thing is stated on screen.

## Qingyun: Taiji Xuanqing Dao

The English subtitle track uses the pinyin stage names: Yuqing, Shangqing, Taiqing. The usual translations are
Jade Purity, Upper Purity and Grand Purity.

| Stage | 汉字 | Subs | Also called | What the episodes show | First stated |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yuqing, levels 1–3 | 玉清 | Yuqing | Jade Purity | Gathering and circulating qi. Xiaofan takes years to break the first level (EP3) and practises the third from Tian Ling'er's copy (EP5). | EP3 |
| Yuqing, levels 4–6 | 玉清 | Yuqing | Jade Purity | Enough to drive a treasure with qi. Lin Jingyu reaches level 6 in five years (EP3), Tian Ling'er is at 6 (EP4), Xiaofan at 4 for the tournament (EP7–8). | EP3 |
| Yuqing, levels 7–9 | 玉清 | | | Nobody on screen is stated to be here. | — |
| Shangqing | 上清 | (Indonesian only) | Upper Purity | Lin Jingyu after ten years with Wan Jianyi (EP34). | EP34 |
| Taiqing | 太清 | Taiqing | Grand Purity | The demon leaders ask whether Dao Xuan has reached it when he raises the Zhu Xian Sword Formation (EP25). The sword drives a weaker user mad. | EP25 |

The levels are called "the n-th level of the Qing Method of Taijixuan" in EP3–8. The word Yuqing first appears in
EP7 ("mastered … to the fourth level of Yuqing").

⚠ The wiki's "Cultivation" tab (Mortal, Earthly, Heavenly, Divine, Immortal, Sage, Saint, God) appears nowhere in
the donghua, so it isn't used.

## The cast ladder (`scripts/build_cast.py`)

`Mortal · Yuqing 1–3 · Yuqing 4–6 · Yuqing 7–9 · Shangqing · Taiqing · Beyond the ladder`
(凡人 · 玉清初 · 玉清中 · 玉清高 · 上清 · 太清 · 超凡). Only realms stated on screen are recorded; "Yuyang realm"
and anything else off the Qingyun ladder has no rung. "Beyond the ladder" is used only for the Beast Deity.
