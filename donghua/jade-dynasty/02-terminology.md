## Name slips to watch for (for `tools/REVIEW.md`)

English track: "Yuki", "Kier", "Qi'er" = Lu Xueqi; "Baguio" = Bi Yao; "Tian not easy" = Tian Buyi; "Shou Shuchang" =
Zeng Shucang; "Aoba" = Qingye; "Gray" = Xiao Hui; "Bottle of Gold" = Jin Ping'er. Name cards are parenthesised
captions, e.g. "(Lianxue Sect, Reverend Yegou)".
Indonesian: "Zheng Shushu" = Zeng Shushu; "Kamu Gou", "Ye Gao" = Ye Gou Daoren; "Peri Jin" = Jin Ping'er; "Li Yan" = Li
Xun; "(Raja Setan)" = the Ghost King; "Tuan Muda Darah" (Young Master Blood) = Gui Li.
