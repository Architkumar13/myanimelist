## The righteous path (正道)

- **Qingyun Sect** (青云门): leader Dao Xuan; peaks Tongtian (Dao Xuan, Xiao Yicai), Great Bamboo (Tian Buyi, Su Ru,
  seven disciples), Small Bamboo (Shui Yue; Lu Xueqi, Wen Min), Dragon Head (Cang Song → betrays in EP24; Lin Jingyu,
  Qi Hao), Wind Return (Zeng Shucang, Zeng Shushu), Morning Sun (Shang Zhengliang), Falling Rain (Tian Yun). Shang
  Zhengliang and Tian Yun die in the EP23–25 war (14 of 25 elders dead, EP25). Wan Jianyi hides in the back mountain
  until EP66.
- **Tian Yin Temple** (天音寺): Abbot Pu Hong; Pu Zhi (the massacre, EP25), Pu Kong; Fa Xiang, Fa Shan.
- **Fen Xiang Valley** (焚香谷): Yun Yilan, Shangguan Ce, Li Xun, Yan Hong, Lü Shun. Its leaders have a centuries-old
  pact with the Beast Deity for the five clans' holy weapons (EP51, EP54, EP72).
- **Jingang Sect** (金刚门): Shi Tou; related to Tian Yin (EP15).

## The Demon Sect (魔教, "the Holy Sect")

The four great sects meet for the first time in a hundred years in EP21: Ghost King Sect, Ten Thousand Poisons, He
Huan, Longevity Hall. The Blood-Refining Hall is older and smaller.

| Sect | Leaders | Fate through EP86 |
|---|---|---|
| Ghost King Sect | The Ghost King (Wan Renwang); envoys Qing Long, Bai Hu, Zhu Que (You Ji), Xuan Wu; Mr. Ghost | Qing Long dies EP57; Bai Hu killed by Gui Li EP85 |
| Ten Thousand Poisons | Poison God → Qin Wuyan | Poison God dies EP57; Qin Wuyan EP59; destroyed EP60 |
| He Huan | San Miao; Jin Ping'er | San Miao killed by Jin Ping'er EP60 |
| Longevity Hall | Yu Yangzi; Zhou Yin | Zhou Yin dies EP24; Yu Yangzi EP30 |
| Blood-Refining Hall | Black-Hearted Old Man (800 years ago); Nian Laoda, Ye Gou Daoren, Lin Feng | Wiped out by Gui Li EP27 |

## The Southern Border

The Wu people were once one tribe (wiki "Races"). On screen: the Golden Clan (Great Shaman, chief Tu Ma Gu, the Dog
God), the Wood Clan (sorcerer Ahetai, the Bear God), the Fish-Man Clan, and the five holy weapons that keep the Beast
Deity sealed (EP53). Ling Long's seven warriors include Hei Hu and Hei Mu (EP82).

## Legends

Ling Long and the Beast Deity (ancient); Qingye, Qingyunzi, Tian Yin's founder, Wufangzi (10th leader), Tiancheng (17th
leader); the Black-Hearted Old Man and Madam Jinling (800 years ago); Wan Jianyi and Dao Xuan's succession (a hundred
years ago). See `world.js` `eras`.
