## Words worth knowing before they are plot

- **Spiritual pulse** (灵脉). The organ that takes in energy. One is ordinary. Three is gifted. Nine is a legend. His was sealed, which is the entire reason the opening treats him as a failure.
- **Spiritual Seal** (灵印). One brick of an array. Ranks are just counts of bricks.
- **Aura Sea**. Where the divine soul sits once you hit the Spirit Stage.
- **Sovereign Sea**. Replaces the Aura Sea at Sovereign. A world inside the body.
- **Sovereign Celestial Body** (至尊法身). A second body you refine. The Great Solar Undying Body is his first.
- **Bloodline bond**. The contract with Jiu You. It is not a pet. She is trying to take the body, and the power he borrows is hers.
- **Battle will** (战意). Will plus spiritual energy. Useless until there is a troop to synchronise.
- **Lower plane**. A small world under the Great Thousand World. Weaker energy, and a hard ceiling. People who break that ceiling ascend. Two of them are already famous upstairs.
- **Fiend clan**. Not the local "demonic" palaces. They consume the spiritual energy of a land until cultivation there becomes impossible.
