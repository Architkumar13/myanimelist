# 04 · Mu Chen

[← Back to index](README.md)

Young lord of Mu Territory. The donghua starts with him already back from the Spiritual Road, about sixteen, initial Spiritual Movement, and a reputation for a slaughter he is not allowed to explain. Realm-by-realm numbers are in [01-power-system.md](01-power-system.md). This page is the other half: why those numbers understate his fights.

## What he has, in order

### Episodes 1–13
- **A sealed spiritual pulse.** This is why he spent years unable to cultivate like everyone else. The seal coming loose is the start of the story. How many pulses are actually in there is a later reveal, and the number matters: one is normal, nine is the story people tell.
- **The Spiritual Road.** Called the Spiritual Land here. A year inside a place where no energy works, ended by expulsion. Survivors get an empowerment. He also comes back with a black paper. The donghua waits until episode 91 to show what the paper is for.
- **Great Pagoda Art, the yang half.** Called the Great Buddha Art. Episode 2. Left by his mother. It is a cultivation method that also hits and blocks.
- **Spiritual Seals.** Wen Ling tests him. Arrays are his second path, and they are the half of his mother's talent that is not the pagoda. The test falls in the early academy episodes, before the swamp.
- **Jiu You, the Nine Netherbird.** Episode 13, in the Black Eerie Swamp (Dark Abyss). She is bound into his body. The relationship is a bloodline bond, not a summons: she is older, she wants a body, and the power he spends is hers. The donghua gives her a human face while she is still a Spiritual Beast. The novel waits until she evolves.

### Episodes 15–52
- A **seat at Northern Heavens Spiritual Academy**, which is the entire ambition of every serious student in the realm.
- At **Spirit Stage** (episode 28) a swallowed beast soul becomes a technique. The bird had already eaten two Heaven-rank souls on his behalf.
- The **White Dragon treasury** (episodes 44–52) closes season 1. Season 1 adapts novel chapters 1–264.

### Episodes 53–92
- Episode 57: she passes a thunder tribulation and becomes a **Nine Netherworld Bird**, a Divine Beast.
- Episode 61: **Ling Xi** takes over his array training. She knew his mother, which the painting in episode 62 starts to admit.
- Episode 68: the **Great Meru Demonic Pillar** (Great Sumeru Demonic Pillar). Using it drags Demonic Dragon Palace down on the academy.
- Episode 73: **Great Solar Undying Body**, shown at Holy Spirit Mountain. A Sovereign Celestial Body, on screen before he is a Sovereign.
- Episode 78: his mother, as a spiritual body. In this adaptation she kills the Black Dragon Sovereign.
- Episode 79: the **yin half** of the Pagoda Art joins the yang half. He also comes out of the North Heaven Gate and takes Shen Cangsheng's place at the top of the Heavenly Rankings.
- Episode 88: **Human Body Disaster**, off a Divine Spiritual Fruit, mid-tournament.
- Episode 91: the black paper purifies fiend energy. The wiki identifies it as a volume of the **Immortal Catalog**.

## What he does not have yet

- The **Spiritual Energy Disaster** and **Spirit Disaster**. Then Sovereign.
- **Nine pulses**, fully awake.
- **Three Pures**: two further bodies, each able to cultivate its own celestial body. This is the late-novel reason one person can walk the energy path, the array path and the formation path without dropping one.
- **Battle formations.** He starts at 1st grade Sovereign. The aired donghua stops at the first of the three disasters.
- A name on the **Firmament Board**.

## People who matter early

| Who | When | Why they're in the story |
|---|---|---|
| Luo Li | Spiritual Road, then the academy | Princess of the Luo God Clan. She hunted him for half a year inside the trial. The relationship is the spine of the show, and her clan is a Heavenly Sovereign power. |
| Mu Feng | episode 2 | His father. Territory lord. Later, Heavenly Fusion and a unified Northern Spiritual Alliance. |
| Jiu You | episode 13 | The bond. |
| Ji Xuan | a glimpse at episode 60, then episode 61 | The other genius from the Spiritual Road, and the long rivalry. |
| Shen Cangsheng and Li Xuantong | season 2 | The top of the academy until he isn't. |
| Ling Xi | episode 61 | Arrays, and a line back to his mother. |
| Qing Yanjing | mentioned from the start, seen at 70 and 78 | His mother. The source of the pagoda art and the arrays. |

His realm label is the honest number. The fight is usually a phase or a stage above it. That gap is the bird, the art, and the arrays, not a mistake in the subtitles.
