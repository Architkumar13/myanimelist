# 01 · Power System

[← Back to index](README.md)

Three paths. Almost everyone walks only the first.

| Path | Chinese | What it actually is |
|---|---|---|
| Spiritual Energy | 灵力 | Your own energy, cultivated in the body. Required before the other two mean anything. |
| Spiritual Array | 灵阵 | You spend Spiritual Seals to shape the energy already in the world. Talent-gated. Mu Chen's mother left him this. |
| Battle Formation | 战阵 | One commander, and the fused will of a troop. In older ages this was just a branch of arrays. He does not start it until Sovereign. |

A fourth practice, tempering the physical body, sits underneath Spiritual Energy. It is how you survive the first of the three disasters, not a separate ladder.

## Spiritual Energy

Names in the second column are the ones used here. The donghua's English does not rename the realms much. It renames techniques and places. See [02-terminology.md](02-terminology.md).

### 1. Sensing (感应境)
Feel the world's spiritual energy and try to draw it in. No phases.

### 2. Spiritual Movement (灵动境) — initial, middle, late
Energy stays in the body. Spiritual arts become possible.

Mu Chen is already at the **initial** phase in episode 1. **Middle** in episode 3, in the fight with Liu Yang. **Late** in episode 10.

### 3. Spiritual Rotation (灵轮境) — initial, middle, late
Also called the Spiritual Wheel. The energy condenses into a turning wheel and gets denser. A Rank 1 array master is about this strong.

**Initial** in episode 13, as the Nine Netherbird is bound to him. **Late** in episode 21. His fights at the late phase already look like the next realm.

### 4. Spirit (神魄境) — initial, middle, late
The watershed. A divine soul forms in the Aura Sea, and a beast's soul essence can be swallowed and, later, used.

**Initial** episode 28, **middle** episode 31, **late** episode 34.

### 5. The Three Heavens (三天之境)

| Heaven | Chinese | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Heavenly Fusion | 融天 | You can move the dense energy of the world. A swallowed beast soul shows its real ability. |
| Heavenly Transformation | 化天 | The divine soul blends into the surroundings and borrows them as a shield. |
| Heavenly Completion | 通天 | The divine soul can travel a long way outside the body and not die. |

Mu Chen: Fusion initial in episode 40, middle in episode 46. Transformation initial in episode 54, late in episode 59. Completion initial in episode 70, late in episode 79.

### 6. Three Sovereign Disasters (至尊小三难)
The gate in front of Sovereign. A lot of geniuses die here.

1. **Human Body Disaster** (肉身难) — the body. He reaches it in episode 88.
2. **Spiritual Energy Disaster** (灵力难) — the energy. Not aired yet.
3. **Spirit Disaster** (神魄难) — three grades of spirit fire. Not aired yet.

### 7. Sovereign (至尊境) — 1st to 9th grade
The mark of a real expert in the Great Thousand World. Three things define it: the **Sovereign Sea** (the old energy sea shatters and a hidden world replaces it, so the soul survives the body's death), **Sovereign spiritual energy**, and a **Sovereign Celestial Body**.

Grades 1–4 are mostly "how much energy." From the **5th grade** the difference is space: crossing it, then cracking it, then twisting it, then breaking it. The deans of the Five Great Academies are Sovereigns. Mu Chen is not one yet. The Great Solar Undying Body shown in episode 73 is the shape of a celestial body, refined properly only after he arrives.

### 8. Earth Sovereign (地至尊)
Lesser, Greater, Perfected, then a state the wiki calls Touching Heavenly Sovereign, which is still Earth Sovereign. Spiritual energy crystallises. Mountains and rivers can be raised by hand. A Perfected Earth Sovereign's sea is large enough to kill anyone below the realm.

### 9. Heavenly Sovereign (天至尊)
Spirit grade, Immortal grade, Saint grade. Each has initial, middle and late. The flesh can turn into a spiritual body. Saint grade commands the world's energy inside a domain, and that is the usual meaning of "the strongest person in the room" until the last stretch of the novel.

### 10. Ruler (主宰境), then Great Ruler (大主宰境)
Past Heavenly Sovereign you have to be acknowledged by the World's Will. The **Firmament Board** appears. Writing your **surname** on it makes you a Ruler and grants a share of the world's power. Writing your **given name** is far harder and makes you a Great Ruler, with the world's power itself. That second stroke is the title of the book.

## Spiritual Arrays

Ranked by how many **Spiritual Seals** (灵印) you can condense and control at once. You need Spiritual Movement before you can learn. Pre-made array seeds exist because a real array is slow to set up.

| Rank | Seals | Roughly matches |
|---|---|---|
| Preliminary | under 15 | below Spiritual Rotation |
| Master, rank 1 | 15 | Spiritual Rotation |
| Master, rank 2 | 21 | Spirit |
| Master, rank 3 | — | Heavenly Fusion |
| Master, rank 4 | 100 | Heavenly Transformation |
| Master, rank 5 | 201 | Heavenly Completion through the three disasters |
| Grandmaster | Human / Earth / Heaven | the nine Sovereign grades, three each |
| Scholar | Human / Earth / Heaven | Lesser, Greater, then Perfected Earth Sovereign |
| Ancestor | Spirit / Immortal / Saint | the three Heavenly Sovereign grades |

An Ancestor casually condenses tens of thousands of seals and can build a world that treats every visitor as an enemy. Qing Yanjing, Mu Chen's mother, is a Saint-grade Spiritual Array Ancestor.

## Battle Formations

Ranked by runes in the **Spirit of Battle Will**. Battle will is willpower fused with spiritual energy. One master, plus a troop. The slowest path to activate and the highest ceiling.

| Stage | Roughly matches |
|---|---|
| Learning, under 10,000 runes | below 7th grade Sovereign |
| Ten-thousand runes | about 7th grade Sovereign |
| Hundred-thousand | about 9th grade Sovereign |
| Million | Earth Sovereign |
| Ten million | Heavenly Sovereign |
| The will of the whole Great Thousand World | Great Ruler, and a formation master |

He begins this path at 1st grade Sovereign. None of it is in the aired episodes.

## How to read his fights

The realm on his profile and the realm he can hit are not the same number. The wiki's combat column sits about a phase, sometimes a whole stage, above the realm he just broke into, because of the bird, the pagoda art, and the arrays. When someone "a realm above him" loses, that gap is the reason.
