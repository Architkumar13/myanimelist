## The stack

**Lower Planes** sit under the **Great Thousand World** (大千世界). The upper plane is larger and far richer in spiritual energy. A Sovereign, who would be a myth downstairs, is only "a real expert" upstairs. The donghua often translates the upper plane as the **Great World**.

Anyone who ascends had to be the strongest person in their own world and stubborn past the point of sense. The two the story keeps pointing at are the **Flame Emperor** (Xiao Yan, from *Battle Through the Heavens*) and the **Martial Ancestor** (Lin Dong, from *Martial Universe*). Season 2 shows the first at episode 53 and the second in a flashback at episode 59. Their books are not required to follow this one.

Above even Heavenly Sovereigns, the **Firmament Board** is how the world itself keeps score. That is endgame. It is not in the aired episodes.

## Where he starts

The **Northern Spiritual Realm** (北灵境) is one realm of the **Hundred Spirit Heavens**, on the **Hundred Spirit Continent**. An ordinary continent. The realm is split into **nine territories**, held in a deliberate balance:

- **Mu Territory** (the donghua says Mu Realm) — his father Mu Feng
- **Liu Territory** — the rivals, Liu Qingtian and his son Liu Yang
- Tang, Hong, Lie, Luo
- A Bai Territory, named in episode 21 of this donghua and left unnamed in the novel
- Two more the sources don't bother to name

**Northern Spiritual Academy** stays neutral and is still the most important building in the realm, because it holds the quotas for the Five Great Academies. East and West branches. Each has an Earth Class (Sensing) and a Heaven Class (Spiritual Movement and up).

Outside the nine lords: **Mang Yin Mountain**, the strongest underworld force, later destroyed, and **Nine Dragon City**.

## The Five Great Academies (五大院)

Episode 15 is when they stop being a rumour. Geniuses from clans that could teach their own children still travel across continents to attend. Each academy is named for the ancient divine beast that guards it, and each dean is a Sovereign.

| Academy | Guardian |
|---|---|
| Northern Heavens Spiritual Academy | Northern Sea Dragon |
| Myriad Phoenix Spiritual Academy | a phoenix, as the name says |
| Saint Spiritual Academy | |

The deans, in the order the wiki lists them: Tai Cang, Tang Qiu, Tian Sheng, Wu Tianwang, Tian Song. A hundred years ago **Nine Cauldrons Spiritual Academy** held one of the five seats and lost it.

They jointly own the **Mirror of Judgement**, which is what picks Spiritual Road candidates, and they jointly run that trial.

## Northern Heavens Continent (北苍大陆)

On screen from episode 25. An ordinary continent that happens to host one of the five academies. The other powers a viewer meets in season 1 and 2:

- **Demonic Dragon Palace** — Black Dragon Sovereign. They were close to ruling the continent until the White Dragon Sovereign stole a treasure. The grudge is the siege in episodes 76–78.
- **Western Extreme Palace**
- The chambers of commerce: Nine Summers, Umbra, Heavenly Yuan
- **Heavenly Cauldron Divine Sect**, **Mysterious Yin Mountain**

The young experts of those powers are the field at Holy Spirit Mountain (episodes 71–76).

## Who is actually strong

Read this from the bottom. The top two rows are not in the donghua yet, except as names and one spiritual body.

| Tier | Who | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| Cities under a territory | Mu City and its lords | Spiritual Rotation |
| A realm | Nine Territories, Northern Spiritual Academy | Heavenly Fusion is already a big deal. Mu Feng's later unification of the nine sits around the novel's chapter 451. |
| A continent | Demonic Dragon Palace and its allies | Sovereign |
| The five academies | Deans, elders, a divine beast | Sovereign, with a beast that is older than the dean |
| Great clans | Luo God Clan, Ancient Buddha Clan | Heavenly Sovereign |
| The board | A Ruler, then a Great Ruler | The world's own power |

**Luo Li** is a princess of the Luo God Clan. She is not, in the opening, anywhere near her clan's ceiling. She is simply the reason that ceiling will eventually notice a boy from Mu Territory.

**Qing Yanjing** is his mother, of the Ancient Buddha Clan, and a Saint-grade Spiritual Array Ancestor. Episode 2 mentions her. Episode 70 is a glimpse. Episode 78 is a spiritual body, long enough to save him and kill the Black Dragon Sovereign, not long enough to stay.
