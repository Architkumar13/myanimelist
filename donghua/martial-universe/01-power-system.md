# 01 · Power system

Everything here is checked against the 72 episode reviews (`data/episodes/review/`). "EP" is the continuous episode
number. Realm names follow the fan wiki (the Wuxiaworld novel translation), because the official English track renders
them differently every time ("Shade", "Shaper", "Atmospheric environment" for 造形境 and 造气境).

## Yuan Power realms (元力境界)

"steal Yin and Yang, seize Creation, turn Nirvana, grasp life and death" (窃阴阳，夺造化，转涅槃，握生死).

## Symbol Masters (符师): the second ladder

Mental Strength (精神力, subs "mental strength") is trained separately. A Symbol Master engraves runes and symbols and
fights with them. The donghua's own ladder:

| Rank | 汉字 | Lin Dong at | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st-5th Seal Symbol Master | 一印…五印符师 | 1st Seal EP6, 2nd EP11, 3rd EP16, 4th by EP26 | Master Yan is 4th Seal (EP8). Each seal is a big step: the Symbol Master Tower's 8th floor is rare even for 3rd Seals (EP15). |
| Spirit Symbol Master | 灵符师 | Mental Strength at "advanced Spirit Symbol Master" level after the Devouring Ancestral Symbol (EP43) | Master Yan names it as the rank after the 5th Seal (EP12). Needed to forge Soul Treasures (EP18). |
| Heaven Symbol Master | 天符师 | EP56 (refining the Heavenly Symbol Spirit Tree); 4th Seal by EP72 | Advanced Spirit Symbol Masters can enter it with the Spirit Tree (EP54). Each seal is a "wind and thunder" tribulation: he takes 1st → 4th Seal in one fight (EP72). |

The novel goes on to Soul and Divine Symbol Masters and Symbol Grandmasters (the wiki's ranks); none is named on
screen yet.

Symbol Master tools on screen: Destiny Symbol (本命符印) and the Ancient Swirl Symbol (古漩符印, EP6/EP8), Mental
Grindstone (EP9), Spiral Wave (EP9), Materialized Spirit Needle (EP6), Huasheng Symbol Formation (EP15), Symbol
Puppets in low, mid and high grades (符傀, EP20, EP35), Mental Energy Fire (精神之火, EP39).

## Grades

- **Martial arts (武学):** graded 1st-grade (一品) upward (EP2: Eight Desolation Palm is 2nd grade); then superior
  (上乘, EP26), Manifestation-grade (造化武学, EP35-37) and spiritual martial arts (灵武学, EP65: Great Desolate
  Heaven-Imprisoning Hand).
- **Soul Treasures (灵宝):** low, mid and high grade (EP19-30: the Ancient Heavenly Scales Halberd goes from an
  unfinished mid grade to high grade in EP30), then Earth-grade (地阶, Heavy Prison Peak, EP48). The wiki continues
  with Sky-grade, Pure Yuan treasures and Divine Items.
- **Elixirs (灵药):** graded by number (5th-grade Void Spirit Grass, EP8; 6th grade, EP17). Pills used as currency:
  Yang Yuan Pills (EP4), Pure Yuan Pills (EP19), Nirvana Pills (EP34; made only by Nirvana experts).
- **Yuan Dan stars:** a Yuan Dan has one to nine stars (Lin Zhentian four, EP6; Lin Dong eight, EP11).

## Bodies

The Great Sun Thunder Body (大日雷体): Copper Thunder Body (EP26) → Jade Thunder Body (EP34) → perfected (EP46).
Demon Ape Transformation with Ancient Dragon Ape blood (EP30). Yuan Essence Power, Yuan Power fused with Mental
Strength (EP31). Nirvana Golden Body (EP53).
