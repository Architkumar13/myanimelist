# 03 · World and powers

Checked against the episode reviews. "EP" is the continuous episode number. `scripts/make_world.py` holds the same
facts as data (regions, factions, tiers, ruins); this note is the readable version and the record of decisions.

- ⚠ The show never gives a map or distances inside the Great Yan Dynasty. `show.js` `map` is a sketch that only keeps
  the order of the road (Qingyang → Yan City → Sky Flame Mountains / Dayang → Great Desolate Province → Lin City →

  Great Yan Province. It isn't a separate region on the site.
- The Ancient Treasure Trove is about a month's journey from the Demon Forest (EP60).

## Who outranks whom (tiers, weakest first)

1. **Town families** (Lin, Lei, Xie): heads at Heavenly Yuan to Yuan Dan initial (EP6).
2. **City powers** (Thousand Gold Association, Blood Wolf Gang, Symbol Master Guild, Blood Cloth Sect, Ghost Blade
   Sect, Eagle Martial Dojo…): leaders at Yuan Dan perfect, sometimes a 4th Seal Symbol Master.
3. **Province sects** of Great Desolate Province (Yin Puppet Sect, Great Demon Sect, Martial Alliance): several
   Manifestation elders (EP40-41).
4. **The Four Great Clans** (Lin, Wang, Qin, Huangpu, EP17-18, EP33): a Nirvana patriarch (Lin Fan, EP48).
5. **Dynasties** (Great Yan, Holy Light, Xuanbing, Sky Eagle, Youquan, Primordial, Heavenly Demon): a Nirvana
   emperor (Mo Jingtian, One Yuan, EP49).
6. **Super dynasties** (Demon Rock, Fengyun): many Nirvana fighters; their young elite reach Four to Six Yuan.
7. **Super Sects** (Dao Sect, Yuan Gate, Nine Heavens Taiqing Palace): they pick the Hundred Dynasties' best. The
   strongest person on screen, Zheng Zhong of the Western Xuan Region, is Profound Life (EP72).

## Ancients and ruins (the "Ancients" and "Ruins" pages)

The show has no eras of emperors. Its past is dead sects and old masters: the expert who once held all eight
Ancestral Symbols (EP23), the Black-Eyed Elder (previous master of the Devouring Ancestral Symbol, EP35), the tomb's
Nirvana master (EP21), Tian Ze of the sect inside the Ancient Tablet (EP38), the Eight Extremes Sect (EP63-65), and
Qing Zhi the Green Dragon King (EP66).

Ruins in the order he enters them: Celestial Dan Pool (EP9), Symbol Master Tower (EP13-16), the Nirvana tomb
(EP18-22), Great Desolate Ancient Tablet (EP33-38), Xuanyin Crack (EP42-44), Sacred Spirit Pool (EP50), Leiyan
Valley (EP54-56), Ancient Treasure Trove (EP61-66), Baichao Mountain (EP70-72).

## Novel-only, left out

The Yimo war and the Devil Prison, the Symbol Ancestor (the novel's maker of the Stone Talisman), the other six
Ancestral Symbols by name, the Palace of Darkness, and everything about the Great Yan Dynasty's history before EP1.
