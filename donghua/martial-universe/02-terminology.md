# 02 · Terminology

What the show calls things. **Name** is what this companion uses. **Official subs** is the official YouTube English
track's most common wording (it's machine-quality and varies, so it's a guide, not a rule). **Also** gives the novel
translation (the fan wiki) and other renderings. **First** is the first episode the word is used on screen.

Rule: where the official English is consistent (Little Mink, Great Yan Dynasty, Stone Talisman, Blood Wolf Gang) it
wins; otherwise the wiki's title does. This table is the source of `world.js` `terms`.

## Name decisions

- 灵符师 is **Spirit Symbol Master**: the donghua names it as the rank after the 5th Seal (EP12) and before 天符师 (EP54).
  The novel's "Soul Symbol Master" is 魂符师, which the show hasn't used.
- 造化境 is **Manifestation** (wiki); 造化三境 (Form, Qi, Manifestation) is "the three Creation realms".
- 魔岩王朝 is **Demon Rock Dynasty**: the official English says it every time.
- Little Flame (小炎) is a Fire Python Tiger (火蟒虎, EP3/EP18); his human name from EP59 is Lin Yan (林炎).
- Little Mink (貂爷/小貂) is a Celestial Demon Marten (天妖貂, EP8) living in the Stone Talisman; human form EP66.
