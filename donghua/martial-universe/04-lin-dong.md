## Journey stages

### Qingyang Town (EP1-3)

- **Stone Talisman** (石符, EP1): It heals him and teaches him in its dream space, which is how a beaten 'son of trash' starts to climb.
- **Tong Bei Fist, ten rings** (通背拳, EP1): Copying the shadow's ten-ring fist lifts him two layers in one night.
- **Eight Desolation Palm** (八荒掌, EP2): His father's 2nd-grade palm, which he soon uses better than the family expected.
- **Yin Pearl** (阴珠, EP3): Its Yin Sha energy is what lets him reach Earthly Yuan in the duel with Lei Li.
- **Little Flame** (小炎, EP3): The sickly Fire Python Tiger cub he picks in the Hunting Battle grows into his fighting partner.

### Ironwood Manor and Yan City (EP4-10)

- **Qimen Seal** (奇门印, EP4): A 3rd-grade fragment that beats Lei Li's Rushing Thunder Light and stays his main defence for many episodes.
- **Yang Yuan Stone vein** (阳元石, EP4): The vein under Ironwood Manor pays for everything that follows, refined into Yang Yuan Pills by the talisman.
- **Qiankun Bag** (乾坤袋, EP5): Bought for nine Yang Yuan Pills; from now on he carries his pills and loot with him.
- **Divine Movement Chapter** (神动篇, EP5): Master Yan's book opens the Symbol Master path.
- **Yuan-Shattering Shuttle** (碎元梭, EP5): Taken from Wu Sha; once he is a Symbol Master he flies it with his mind.
- **Destiny Symbol, 1st Seal** (本命符印, EP6): Makes him a Symbol Master, a second ladder of strength beside his realm.
- **Ancient Swirl Symbol** (古旋符印, EP6): A rare Destiny Spirit Symbol that absorbs other mental power.
- **Materialized Spirit Needle** (化神针, EP6): Gu Ying's mental needle, later the trick that frees Qing Tan.
- **Little Mink** (貂爷, EP8): The Celestial Demon Marten in the talisman becomes his teacher and guide.
- **Mental Grindstone and Spiral Wave** (精神磨盘 / 尖螺波, EP9): Little Mink's training and attack win him the Celestial Dan Pool contest.
- **Qi-Transforming Spirit Vortex** (化气精旋, EP10): Lets him absorb the pool far faster than the Qingyuan Art and restores the Stone Talisman.
- **Yin Yang Pearls and the Three Yang Art** (阴阳珠 / 三阳决, EP10): The materials for his Yuan Dan before the Wei Tong duel.

### Yan City, the tower and the ancient tomb (EP11-24)

- **Eight-star Yuan Dan** (八星元丹, EP11): Twice his grandfather's four stars, a foundation stronger than most of his rivals'.
- **Mysterious Ice Swords and Lihuo Sword** (冰玄剑 / 离火剑, EP13): Weapons won from Cao Zhu and Liu Long that he steers with his mind.
- **Huasheng Symbol Formation** (化生符阵, EP15): A Qi-grade Secret Spirit Skill from the tower's eighth floor; its Huasheng Light kills Gui Yan.
- **Map of two Ancestral Symbols** (祖符, EP16): Sets his long-term goal beyond the Lin Clan Gathering.
- **Blood Vermillion Devil Fruit** (妖血朱果, EP17): Xuan Su's gift for the 3rd Seal; it takes him to Yuan Dan advanced.
- **Ancient Heavenly Scales Halberd** (天鳞古戟, EP19): An unfinished Soul Treasure bound with his blood; it becomes his main weapon.
- **23,000 Pure Yuan Pills** (纯元丹, EP19): The tomb's hoard fuels his Symbol Puppets and the talisman.
- **Mid-grade Symbol Puppet** (中等符傀, EP20): A Form Creation-level strike he can call on, at 2,000 pills a blow.
- **Extreme Yin Dragon Saliva** (极煞阴龙涎, EP20): The very Yin treasure that lets Qing Tan form her Yin Dan.
- **Nirvana Heart's power** (涅槃心, EP21): Shared with Ling Qingzhu; he wakes at Yuan Dan perfect.
- **Heavenly Scales Halberd Art** (天鳞戟法, EP21): The tomb master's halberd technique, first used against Wang Yan.

### Great Desolate Province and the Ancient Tablet (EP25-36)

- **Copper Thunder Body** (铜雷体, EP26): The first layer of the Great Sun Thunder Body, formed with the stolen Thunder Source.
- **200,000 Pure Yuan Pills** (纯元丹, EP26): Paid by Di Teng and Liu Kui to end the fight, enough to run his puppets for a long time.
- **Subduing Golden Platform Magic Palm** (金台镇魔掌, EP27): A Wang Clan art he uses better than Wang Yan himself.
- **Demon Ape Transformation** (魔猿变, EP30): Made far stronger by Ancient Dragon Ape blood, and it stacks with the Thunder Body.
- **High-grade halberd** (天鳞古戟, EP30): Grand Master Cheng refines the halberd into a high-grade Soul Treasure.
- **Prayer mat and Yuan Essence Power** (神秘蒲团 / 元精之力, EP31): Fuses Yuan Power and Mental Strength into one force that breaks armour.
- **Myriad Beast Fruit** (万兽果, EP32): Refined to push his cultivation and Thunder Body further.
- **Jade Thunder Body** (玉雷体, EP34): The Yin Wind Formation forges the second layer.
- **Nirvana Pill** (涅槃丹, EP34): A dead disciple's gift, meant for when he nears Nirvana.
- **High-grade Symbol Puppet** (高等符傀, EP35): Taken from Elder Cao; strong enough to break a sect formation.
- **The previous master's approval** (吞噬祖符, EP35): The Black-Eyed Elder gives him the means to break the Ancestral Symbol's seal.

### The Devouring Ancestral Symbol (EP37-43)

- **Great Desolate Heaven-Imprisoning Finger** (大荒囚天指, EP37): The ancient sect's Manifestation martial art, his strongest attack through EP72.
- **Lin Langtian's bag of Nirvana Pills** (乾坤袋, EP38): Lands in his hands when Lin Langtian leaves him to die.
- **Mental Energy Fire** (精神之火, EP39): Turns the fight with the Half-step Manifestation Cao Zhen.
- **Purple Shadow Nine Breaks** (紫影九破, EP40): A 9th-grade art taken from Teng Lei.
- **Devouring Ancestral Symbol** (吞噬祖符, EP43): He becomes its heir; the Power of Devouring absorbs energy and attacks and makes them his.

### The Yin Puppet Sect and the Lin Clan Gathering (EP44-48)

- **Half-step Manifestation** (半步造化, EP44): The Devouring Ancestral Symbol turns the Xuanyin Crack's cold into Yuan Power.
- **Great Desolate Sea-Splitting Seal** (大荒分海印, EP44): Another Great Desolate technique, used on Teng Sha and later Lin Langtian.
- **Blood Spirit Puppet** (血灵傀, EP45): A Nirvana-level puppet kept under contract in the Stone Talisman.
- **Great Sun Thunder Body perfected** (大日雷体大成, EP46): His body is ready to face Lin Langtian.
- **Heavy Prison Peak** (重狱峰, EP48): An Earth-grade Soul Treasure from the Lin Clan vault, bound with blood and the talisman.
- **Lin Clan championship** (林氏族比, EP48): Brings the Yan City branch back into the clan and wins him a Battle of Seeds place.

### The Imperial City and the Ancient Battlefield (EP49-59)

- **Hundred Dynasties Battle place** (百朝大战, EP49): One of the Great Yan Dynasty's five places.
- **Heavenly Crocodile Bone Spear** (骨枪, EP50): The Ancient Heavenly Crocodile's Soul Treasure; the emperor lets him keep it.
- **Five fingers** (大荒囚天指, EP53): The Great Desolate Heaven-Imprisoning Finger's fifth finger breaks the Holy Light Formation.
- **Saint Elephant Sky Crumbling Tackle** (圣象崩天撞, EP53): Learned from Jin Mu's own attack.
- **Nirvana Golden Body** (涅槃金身, EP53): A Manifestation-grade body, forged with ten thousand Nirvana Pills.
- **Ancient Secret Key** (远古秘钥, EP56): One of the four keys to the Ancient Treasure Trove; it also teleports him to safety.
- **Heaven Symbol Master** (天符师, EP56): The Heavenly Symbol Spirit Tree lifts him to the next Symbol Master ladder.
- **Wind Thunder Seal** (风雷印, EP57): Beats Chen Mu at the prisoner exchange.
- **Devouring Thunder Palm** (吞噬雷掌, EP58): His own art, joining Mental Strength and the Power of Devouring.
- **Lin Yan** (林炎, EP59): Little Flame passes the Nirvana Tribulation, takes human form and becomes his sworn brother.

### Nirvana and the Hundred Dynasties Battle (EP60-72)

- **One Yuan Nirvana** (一元涅槃, EP60): Passed in the Pill River, which also meets the battle's rule that only Nirvana fighters go on.
- **Life and Death Reincarnation Pill** (生死转轮丹, EP62): The pill Little Mink needs to rebuild his body.
- **Great Desolate Heaven-Imprisoning Hand** (大荒囚天手, EP65): A spiritual martial art from the Eight Extremes trial; it kills Shi Kun.
- **Green Heaven Materialized Dragon Skill** (化龙决, EP66): Qing Zhi's gift: dragon scale shield, Immovable Bell and dragon form.
- **Three Yuan Nirvana** (三元涅槃, EP66): Three months in the Green Dragon Hall's pill store.
- **Black Dragon Sky-Roaring Seal** (黑龙啸天印, EP68): A Heaven-grade Soul Treasure taken from Feng Cang.
- **Four Yuan Nirvana** (四元涅槃, EP69): Reached mid-fight against Song Zhen.
- **4th Seal Heaven Symbol Master** (四印天符师, EP72): Three tribulations in one fight on Baichao Mountain.
- **Champion and a place in the Dao Sect** (道宗, EP72): The end of the Hundred Dynasties Battle and the start of his super sect path.

## Bodies

- EP11 **Twenty-two meridians and an eight-star Yuan Dan** (八星元丹): With the Three Yang Art and the Stone Talisman, Lin Dong opens twenty-two meridians. The Yin Yang Pearls then give him an eight-star Yuan Dan, twice his grandfather's four stars (EP11).
- EP25 **Great Sun Thunder Body** (大日雷体): On the road to Great Desolate Province he trains the body art by calling lightning down on a snowy peak. One meridian is still closed, so he cannot yet form its first layer (EP25).
- EP26 **Copper Thunder Body** (铜雷体): The first layer of the Great Sun Thunder Body. He forms it by refining the Thunder Source he stole from under Di Teng, and breaks through to Form Creation at the same time (EP26).
- EP30 **Demon Ape Transformation** (魔猿变): He finds the art on a bone slip among Luo Jiu's things (EP28) and draws blood essence from the Manifestation-level Ancient Dragon Ape (EP29). Refining that blood gives him a far stronger transformation than Luo Jiu's, and he can combine it with the Copper Thunder Body (EP30).
- EP34 **Jade Thunder Body** (玉雷体): The winds of a Yin Wind Formation inside the Great Desolate Ancient Tablet nearly tear him apart and push the Copper Thunder Body up to the Jade Thunder Body (EP34).
- EP46 **Great Sun Thunder Body perfected** (大日雷体大成): After two months of training on the way to Lin City he perfects the Great Sun Thunder Body (EP46). He uses it against Lin Langtian (EP48) and to hold down the Eight Extremes master's palm force (EP64).
- EP53 **Nirvana Golden Body** (涅槃金身): Little Mink teaches him the Lesser Nirvana Golden Body, a Manifestation-grade body art, and burns about ten thousand Nirvana Pills to forge it (EP53). He uses it to push past Hua Yun's men (EP54) and to escape Li Pan (EP55).
- EP71 **Green Dragon form** (化龙决): Qing Zhi gives him the Green Heaven Materialized Dragon Skill in the Green Dragon Hall (EP66). Against Lin Langtian he uses the force of his own Green Dragon Immovable Bell to call the Green Dragon's spirit and take dragon form (EP71).

## Companions

- EP1 **Qing Tan**: His adopted sister. The Stone Talisman draws out her cold poison (EP2), and the Extreme Yin Dragon Saliva cures it (EP24). He leaves her with the family in Yan City when he sets out (EP24). She reaches Qi Creation on her own (EP45) and wins her match at the Lin Clan Gathering (EP47).
- EP3 **Little Flame**: The sickly Fire Python Tiger cub from the Hunting Battle. It evolves on the Thunder Source Crystal Beast's essence (EP26), takes in Ancient Heavenly Crocodile blood (EP50) and becomes Lin Yan, a sworn brother in human form (EP59).
- EP8 **Little Mink**: A Celestial Demon Marten living in the Stone Talisman as a demon spirit. It rebuilds a body in human form and is called Lin Diao (EP66).
- EP45 **Blood Spirit Puppet**: A Nirvana-level spirit puppet sealed in the Stone Talisman under contract.
- EP54 **Mo Ling**: The third prince of the Great Yan Dynasty (EP49). He travels with Lin Dong to Leiyan Valley (EP54) and is freed from the Demon Rock Dynasty (EP57). (until EP60)
- EP68 **Su Rou**: A Daqing Dynasty fighter carrying a sealed inheritance; she joins his group after he saves her twice.
